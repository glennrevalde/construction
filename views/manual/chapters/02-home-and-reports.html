The Home module is where your work starts: the Home Dashboard with the day's figures and reminders, the tools that look things up (Search and Find), and the pages that open and keep reports (the Report Center, Memorized Reports and Saved Report Layouts).

This chapter also holds Working with reports, which explains the tools that every report in the program shares: the dates and filters, sorting, drilling down, printing and PDF, export to a spreadsheet, e-mailing a link, memorizing, scheduled e-mail, and designing the report's layout. The report sections of the other chapters describe only what is special to each report and refer back here.

Home Dashboard

The Home Dashboard is the page that opens after you log in. It shows the key figures of the business, a button for every transaction you may enter, and the reminders that need your attention.

Where to find it: Home > Home Dashboard. It also opens when you log in, and when you click GSMaRT Construction System at the top left of any page.

Step by step: start a transaction from the Home page

  1. Find the module in Quick actions, for example Customers.
  2. Click the button of the transaction, for example Create Invoices.

For estimates, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, statement charges, credit memos, bills, purchase orders, checks and journal entries, a blank form opens at once. The other buttons open the page itself.

Step by step: see the detail behind a figure

  1. Click the blue line under the figure.
  2. The page behind it opens: - under Accounts Receivable: the A/R Aging Summary, - under Accounts Payable: Pay Bills, - under Sales this month (net of VAT): Sales by Item Summary for this month, - under Items at or below reorder point: Inventory Stock Status by Item.

If you may not open that page, the line is plain text instead of a link.

Step by step: deal with a reminder

  • Memorized transactions due: click Open on the card. The Memorized Transactions list opens, where you enter each one. The card shows Due or Overdue beside each date.
  • Asset maintenance due: click Open. Preventive Maintenance opens on the tasks that are due. A task shows, for example, 3 days overdue or Due in 20 days, and stays on the card until someone acknowledges that it is complete.
  • Cash and bank accounts: click an account to open its register in Use Register.

Good to know

  • The figures are worked out when the page opens. To bring them up to date, open the Home page again.
  • The receivables and payables figures are ledger balances. They include opening balances, journal entries to those accounts, and transactions of every date, future-dated ones too.
  • The sales figure leaves out voided, pending and suspended sales. Credit memos and POS returns are not taken off it; the sales reports show the net figure.
  • To-do reminders are added as notes, with the To-do box ticked, in the Customer Center and the Vendor Center. The card shows up to ten, the earliest due first; a due date that has passed is shown in red. When nothing is open it says No open reminders.
  • The bell in the top bar and the pop-up after log-in show the same maintenance tasks as the Asset maintenance due card.

Search

Search finds a customer, a supplier, an item or a document from any word or number you remember: part of a name, a document number, a reference or a barcode.

Where to find it: the search box in the top bar (type and press Enter). Also Home > Find > Search names and items. Search is not listed in the menu.

The results are grouped by kind. Each line is a link.

What Search looks in

Group Looks in Opens
Customers Name, company name, e-mail, phone The customer in the Customer Center
Vendors Name, company name, e-mail The supplier in the Vendor Center
Items Item code, item name, or the whole barcode The item's form in the Item List
Transactions Number, reference, memo, receipt number, payee The transaction

Beside each result is a short description: the company name and phone of a customer or supplier, the type of an item, and the date, name and total of a transaction.

Step by step: search

  1. Click the search box in the top bar.
  2. Type what you remember, for example ABC, INV-00003 or a barcode.
  3. Press Enter. The Search page lists what matches.
  4. Click a result to open it.

To search again, type in the box on the Search page and click Search.

Good to know

  • A word matches anywhere in the text: ABC finds ABC Trading Corp. and any document whose number, reference or memo contains ABC. A barcode must be typed in full.
  • Each group shows up to 20 matches, and Transactions shows the 50 most recent. When nothing matches, the page says Nothing found for followed by your words.
  • A group appears only if you may open the page it leads to, and only the kinds of transaction you may open are listed.
  • A transaction is found by its own number, reference, memo or payee, not by the customer's or supplier's name. To list every transaction of a customer, use Find with the customer's name.

Find

Find looks for transactions in every module at once: estimates, orders, invoices, sales receipts, payments, credit memos, bills, supplier debit memos, bill payments, checks, cash vouchers, deposits, transfers, journal entries, inventory adjustments and transfers, builds, processing orders, POS sales and returns, depreciation and disposals. You describe what you know about the transaction, and Find lists every transaction that fits. Use it when you know "something about" a document but not where it is: an amount, a date range, a supplier, an item, the person who entered it.

Where to find it: Home > Find, or the Find button (funnel icon) in the top bar.

Find has two tabs. Simple asks for the usual few things. Advanced offers more than forty filters that can be combined in any way. The Search names and items button in the title bar opens the Search page.

The Simple tab

The Simple tab looks for one kind of transaction by its name, dates, number and amount.

Fields

Field What to enter
Transaction type The kind of transaction, for example Invoice or Bill. Only the kinds whose page you may open are listed.
Name box The customer, supplier or other name, or Any name. Its label follows the type: Customer:Job, Supplier, Payee, Paid to, Customer or Name.
Date from / To The dates to look between. Either one may be left empty.
Number box Part of the document number. Its label follows the type, for example Invoice #, Bill #, Check #, P.O. # or Voucher #.
Amount The exact total of the transaction, or empty for Any amount.

The name box offers only the names that fit the type: customers for sales documents, suppliers for purchase documents, and every kind of name for checks, journal entries and the rest. A customer also finds the transactions of its jobs.

Step by step: find a transaction on the Simple tab

  1. Choose the Transaction type.
  2. Fill in what you know; leave the rest empty.
  3. Click Find. The results appear under the tab.

Reset clears the tab. The Advanced link beside the buttons, like the Advanced tab itself, opens the Advanced tab with the choices of your last search, so you can add more filters to them.

Example

To find the invoice of ₱89,577.60 for ABC Trading Corp., choose Invoice, type abc in Customer:Job and pick the customer, type 89577.60 in Amount and click Find.

The Advanced tab

The Advanced tab combines any number of filters. A transaction is found only when it matches every filter you set.

The tab has three parts:

  • Choose filter on the left lists every filter in alphabetical order. Type in Type to find a filter to shorten the list. A filter that is set is shown in bold with a green tick.
  • The middle shows the filter you clicked, with a line saying what it finds, and the boxes to set it.
  • Current choices on the right lists every filter that is set, with what it is set to. Click a line to open that filter again; click the cross at its end to remove it. With nothing set, it says that Find lists every transaction you are allowed to open, newest first.

How the filters are set

  • Lists to tick (accounts, names, items, types and so on): tick any number of entries. A transaction is found if it matches any one of them. Type in Type to narrow the list to find an entry. The count under the list says how many are ticked, or None ticked: all of them. Clear unticks them all.
  • Short choices (for example Voided or Paid status): pick one. Either (no filter) leaves the filter off.
  • Dates: pick a period in Dates (Today, This week, This month, Last month, This quarter, This fiscal year, Fiscal year to date, Last fiscal year), or type From and To dates, which makes the period Custom. Any date leaves the filter off.
  • Figures (amount, days past due, quantity): pick =, at most, at least or between and type the figure (two figures for between). Any leaves the filter off. The sign of an amount does not matter.
  • Words (memo, number, reference, address and so on): pick contains, is exactly or starts with, and type the words. Number also offers from ... to, for a range of document numbers such as INV-00005 to INV-00010.

Step by step: find transactions on the Advanced tab

  1. Click Advanced.
  2. Click a filter in Choose filter, for example Name.
  3. Set it in the middle, for example tick Supplier: TechDistributors Inc. It appears under Current choices at once.
  4. Click the next filter, for example Date, and set it. Repeat for every filter you need.
  5. Click Find.

Reset clears every filter.

Example

To list every check and bill payment above ₱50,000.00 made this quarter, set Transaction type to Check and Bill Payment, Amount to at least 50000, and Date to This quarter, then click Find.

  • Click a column heading to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order. The arrow beside the heading shows the order. On a phone the headings are folded away and a drop-down (Newest first, Oldest first, Largest amount first and so on) takes their place.

Find Results

Find Results shows what the Find page found as a report, so the list can be printed, saved as PDF, exported, designed and memorized like any other report. A memorized Find Results report runs the same search again each time it is opened.

Where to find it: the Report button above the results on the Find page. It is also listed under Home in the Report Center; opened from there, with no choices, it lists every transaction you are allowed to open.

The report has no filters of its own: the choices come from the Find page, and the line under the title spells them out, for example "Transaction type: Invoice; Date: This month (09/01/2026 to 09/30/2026)". To change the search, go back to Find.

Column What it shows
Date The date of the transaction.
Type The kind of transaction, with (void) after a voided one.
Num The document number, as a link to the transaction.
Name The customer, supplier, other name or payee.
Account The main account, or Account / Item when every line is listed.
Memo The memo, or the line description.
Status An extra column, left out until it is ticked on the Modify Report panel's Columns tab.
Amount The total, or the line amount.

The total line counts the transactions (or lines) found in the Name column and totals the Amount column. The rows are in date order, oldest first; click a heading to sort them otherwise.

Note

The report lists up to 5,000 transactions. When more were found, a note under the title says Only the first 5,000 of the total are listed here. Narrow the search on the Find page to see the rest. The tools of the report are explained in Working with reports.

Report Center

The Report Center lists every report you may open, grouped by module, and your memorized reports. It is the quickest way to find a report when you are not sure which module it belongs to.

Where to find it: Home > Report Center, or the Report Center button on the Home page and on the Memorized Reports page. Its Memorized Reports button opens the list of memorized reports.

What the page shows

  • A line reminding you that every report can be filtered, customized, drilled into, memorized, printed or saved as PDF, exported and e-mailed.
  • Find a report by name: type part of a name, for example "comparative" or "aging", and the cards keep only the reports whose names have every word you typed. The count under the box says how many are shown ("8 of 119 reports"). Clear the box to see them all again.
  • Memorized reports: each report you memorized and each report other users shared, under its group (for example Month-end), with the report it is based on under its name ("Income Statement, shared"). The card appears once there is at least one.
  • A card for each module, listing its reports: Home, Financial Reports (the reports of the Financial Set-up & Reports menu: the trial balances, balance sheets, income statements, the comparative statements, cash flow, ledgers and journal), Fixed Assets, Customers, Vendors, Inventory, Point of Sale, Banking, BIR Reports and System Administration. Large modules divide them under headings such as CUSTOMER AND RECEIVABLES REPORTS and SALES REPORTS.

Step by step: open a report

  1. Open Home > Report Center.
  2. Click the name of the report. It opens with its usual dates and filters.

To open a memorized report, click its button under Memorized reports. It opens with the dates, filters and layout that were memorized.

Good to know

  • Only the reports your role allows are listed, so a module card may be short or missing.
  • The same reports are listed in the menu, under REPORTS in each module.
  • A shared memorized report is listed only if you may open the report it is based on.

Working with reports

Every report in GSMaRT Construction System works the same way. This section explains the tools once; the report sections of the other chapters describe only what is special to each report.

The parts of a report page

Part What it is
Title bar The report's name and its buttons: Print / PDF, Export to spreadsheet, Email link, Modify Report (or Layouts), Memorize and Help.
Filter bar The dates and the other choices of the report, and the Refresh button.
Heading The company name, the report title and the line with the dates and filters, for example 01/01/2026 through 09/27/2026.

Opening a report

  • From the Report Center: Home > Report Center, then click the report.
  • From the menu: open the module, unfold REPORTS, and click the report.
  • From a memorized report: Home > Memorized Reports, or the Memorized reports buttons of the Report Center.
  • From other pages: many pages link to their reports, for example the figures on the Home page, and blue names and numbers inside other reports.

  1. Choose the customer, account, class or other filter.
  2. Click Refresh.
Tip

When the report shows nothing, it says No data for these settings. Widen the dates, or set a filter back to All.

Filters on related records

Besides its own filters, a report can be narrowed by the fields of the records it is made from: the items it lists or sold, and the customers it is about. Every report where they apply has them in its filter bar:

Filter On the reports about What it keeps
Category items: inventory, sales by item, POS sales by item and category, processing The items of one category of the Category List.
Item type items One kind of item: Inventory part, Inventory assembly, Non-inventory part, Service, Other charge and so on. Only the kinds that are in use are offered.
Preferred supplier items The items whose preferred supplier (on the item's form) is the one chosen. Only suppliers that are the preferred supplier of an item are offered.
Customer type customers: receivables, collections, sales, income by customer, open sales orders The customers of one customer type, with their jobs.
Site inventory and sales One inventory site. On Quantity on Hand by Site it keeps that site's column only.

The filters work together: Category Accessories with Preferred supplier TechDistributors Inc. keeps the accessories bought from that supplier. The totals, subtotals and percentages are those of what is shown. The line under the report's title names every filter chosen, for example "As of 10/04/2026, Makati Store, Category Accessories", so a printout says what it covers. A report narrowed by customer type leaves out its agreement with the ledger (the ledger is the total of every customer).

Sorting

On most reports the column headings are links. Click a heading to sort by it; a small triangle shows the order (up for ascending, down for descending). Click the same heading again to reverse the order. Reports divided into groups with subtotals, such as the financial statements, and a few others whose order matters (the check register and the audit reports, for example) keep their own order, and their headings are plain text.

Sort by (with Increasing or Decreasing beside it) is in the filter bar of the sales and income reports: Income by Customer Summary (Qty sold, Sales, Cost of sales, Income / (loss), Margin %), Sales by Item Summary (Qty, Amount, % of sales, COGS, Gross margin, Margin %), Inventory Sales by Site (Qty sold, Sales, Cost of sales), Sales by Item Group and POS Sales by Item Group (Qty, Total). Choosing a column or an order refreshes the report at once, and the heading line says "Sorted by Amount, decreasing". On a report divided into groups, the groups and their totals keep their places and the lines are sorted inside each group; lines without a figure (no cost on a service item, for example) come last whichever the order. Default order puts the report back in its own order. The sort is kept when the report is memorized, and the column headings of these reports sort the same way.

Cost columns are offered in Sort by only to users who may view costs.

Drilling down to the detail

Words and numbers in blue are links to the detail behind them:

  • a document number opens the transaction,
  • an account on a financial statement opens the General Ledger of that account for the same period,
  • a customer, supplier or item name opens a more detailed report or record,
  • on some charts, a figure opens the report behind it.

A link appears only when you may open the page it leads to; otherwise the text is plain.

Charts

A few reports draw charts above their table: the Accounts Receivable Graph (receivables by aging period and each customer's share), POS Sales by Hour (net sales by hour) and the Co-product & By-product Cost Allocation (allocated cost by output item, for users who may view costs). The charts print with the report.

Print / PDF

  1. Set the dates and filters, and click Refresh.
  2. Click Print / PDF. The report opens on a plain page, without menus or filters, with the buttons Print / Save as PDF and Back at the top.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF. The browser's print window opens.
  4. Choose a printer, or choose to save as PDF.
  5. Click Back to return to the report.

The printed page follows the Page settings of the report's layout: paper size, upright or sideways, margins, page numbers and the title at the top of each page (see "Modifying a report" below). Every printout is recorded in the audit trail.

Tip

Page numbers ("Page 1 of 3") and the title repeated on every page are printed by the browser in the page margins. Chrome and Edge do this; other browsers leave them off.

Export to spreadsheet

  1. Set the dates and filters, and click Refresh.
  2. Click Export to spreadsheet. The browser downloads a CSV file named after the report, for example income_statement.csv.
  3. Open it in Excel or any spreadsheet program.

The file holds the columns shown on the screen, in their order and with their headings, the rows shown on the screen, and the grand total. Amounts are plain numbers with two decimals, without commas, so they can be added up in the spreadsheet; dates are written year-month-day. Every export is recorded in the audit trail.

Email link

Email link opens your e-mail program with a new message. The subject is the report's title and the body is the address of the report, exactly as it is on your screen: the same dates, filters and layout.

The person who receives it needs their own log-in and access to the report. If they are not logged in, the address takes them to the log-in page and then to the Home page; once logged in, they click the address again and see the report run afresh with the latest figures. Nothing is attached: to send the figures themselves, save the report as PDF or export it, and attach the file.

Columns (for users who design reports)

Users who may design reports see a Columns button beside Refresh on reports with more than two columns. It is a quick way to leave columns out:

  1. Click Columns.
  2. Tick Hide beside each column to leave out, or untick it to show it again.
  3. Click Refresh.

The Columns tab of the Modify Report panel does the same and more (order, headings, widths, and fields from the related records).

Layouts (for users who do not design reports)

Users without the design permission see the report the way its default layout sets it. When other users have shared layouts for the report, a Layouts button appears in the title bar. Click it and choose a layout, or Standard for the report without any layout. While a layout is in use, the button shows its name.

Memorize

Memorizing saves a report exactly as it is on the screen (the dates choice, the filters, the sort order, the columns and the layout) under a name of your own, so you can open it again with one click, share it, and have it e-mailed on a schedule.

Fields

Field What to enter
Name The name of the memorized report. It starts as the report's title.
Group A group to file it under, for example Month-end or Weekly. Groups sort the list.
Share with other users Tick it to list the report for everyone. Each user can open it only if they may open the report itself.
Send automatically No, Daily, Weekly or Monthly.
Email to The e-mail address the scheduled report goes to.

Step by step: memorize a report

  1. Set the report up: filters, sorting, columns and layout.
  2. Choose the period in Dates as the last step (see the tip below).
  3. Click Memorize.
  4. Change the Name if you wish and type a Group.
  5. Tick Share with other users if others should have it.
  6. To have it e-mailed, choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly in Send automatically and type the address in Email to.
  7. Click Save.

The program says Report memorized. and shows the report again. It is now in Memorized Reports and in the Report Center.

Tip

A period chosen from the Dates list moves with the calendar: a report memorized on This month always shows the current month. Dates in From and To are remembered as they are. After you press Refresh, the From and To dates on the screen are sent with the report and stay fixed. To memorize a moving period, choose it from Dates after setting the other filters, and click Memorize without pressing Refresh again.

Scheduled e-mail

A memorized report with Send automatically set is e-mailed as a spreadsheet file (CSV) attached to a message whose subject is its name and the company name. The e-mails are sent by a program on the server, tools/send_scheduled_reports.php, which your administrator runs once a day (for example with Windows Task Scheduler). On each run:

  • a daily report goes out once a day, a weekly report when a week has passed since the last sending, and a monthly report when a month has passed;
  • a new scheduled report goes out at the next run;
  • the report runs with the access of the user who memorized it, and is skipped if that user's account is no longer active or has lost access to the report;
  • the time of the last sending is shown in the Schedule column of Memorized Reports, and each sending (or failure) is recorded in the audit trail.
Note

Nothing is sent when Email to is empty, or when the administrator has not set the sender up on the server. The attached file has the report's standard columns and all its rows, whatever layout was used on the screen.

Modifying a report

The Modify Report panel, which works like the Modify Report window of QuickBooks, changes how a report looks and what it prints: which columns (the report's own and fields from the records behind each row), which rows, the heading and the foot, the fonts, how the figures are written, and the printed page. It is on every report. It needs the special permission Can design report layouts (Administrators always have it). A modified report can be used just for now, or saved as a layout, shared with the other users and made the one the report opens with.

Click Modify Report in the title bar of any report. The panel opens between the filter bar and the report. Click Modify Report again to close it. While a saved layout is in use, the button reads Modify Report: followed by the layout's name.

The panel has eight tabs: Columns, Rows, Heading, Fonts & look, Figures, Footer, Page and Layouts. Two kinds of change work differently:

  • Fonts, sizes, colours, shading, lines, the words of the heading and the foot, and which heading lines show, change on the report as you pick them. So do showing or hiding a column, its width, its alignment and its heading, for the columns already on the report.
  • Changes to what is printed (moving columns, the rows, the figures, the page) wait. The note Some changes show once you press "Show it this way". appears; click Show it this way to see them.

The buttons at the foot of the panel:

  • Show it this way redraws the report with every choice on the panel. Nothing is saved yet, but the report as shown can be printed, exported, e-mailed and memorized.
  • Back to the plain report opens the report without any layout, keeping its dates and filters.

The Columns tab

The tab lists every column the report can print: first the report's own columns, then the related fields, the fields of the records its rows come from. A row of the Sales by Customer Detail report, for example, comes from a transaction and from a customer, so the memo, the class, the site or the status of the transaction, and the phone, the e-mail, the terms or the credit limit of the customer, can all be added to the report with a tick. The field says which record it comes from: Memo from the transaction, Phone from the customer.

Saving, sharing and deleting a layout

A layout keeps everything set on the Modify Report panel's tabs, the added fields included, so the report can be opened the same way again, by you or by everyone.

Field What it does
Name The layout's name, for example "Trial Balance for the bank".
Replace "..." instead of adding another Shown when the layout that is open is yours, and ticked: saving updates it. Untick to save a new layout.
Open this report with this layout from now on Makes it the layout the report opens with.
Let the other users use it too Shares it. Users who cannot design reports can then choose it from their Layouts button.

Step by step: save a layout

  1. Open the report and click Modify Report.
  2. Make your changes on the tabs. There is no need to click Show it this way first: everything on the panel is saved, including changes not shown yet.
  3. Open the Layouts tab and click Save this layout.
  4. Type a Name.
  5. Tick Open this report with this layout from now on and Let the other users use it too as needed.
  6. Click Save the layout.

The report opens in the new layout, with the message Layout "Trial Balance for the bank" saved. It is on the Modify Report panel of that report.

Default and shared layouts

  • A layout marked as the default opens whenever the report is opened by someone who can see it: only you for a private layout, everyone for a shared one.
  • A report has one default at a time. Making a layout the default takes the mark off the report's other layouts that you can see.
  • To see the report without its default layout, click Back to the plain report on the Modify Report panel, choose Standard under Layouts, or choose Standard (no layout) on the Layouts tab.
  • A layout can be changed only by its owner, and deleted by its owner or an administrator. To adapt someone else's layout, open it, change it and save it under a new name.
Important

Making one of your private layouts the default also takes the mark off a shared layout that was the default for everyone, and nobody else sees your private layout. Everyone else then gets the plain report. Keep the default on a shared layout when others rely on it.

Step by step: delete a layout

  1. Open the report in the layout (Layouts tab > Open a saved layout).
  2. Click Delete followed by the layout's name.
  3. Click Delete it to confirm. The report itself is not changed.

The program says Layout deleted. Layouts can also be deleted on the Saved Report Layouts page.

Message What it means

Good to know about reports

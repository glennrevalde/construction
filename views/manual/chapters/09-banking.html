The Banking module records the money that goes into and out of your bank and cash accounts: checks, cash vouchers paid out of a petty cash or revolving fund, deposits, transfers between accounts, and the monthly bank reconciliation. It also keeps a register for each account, like a checkbook.

In GSMaRT Construction System a bank or cash account is an account of type Bank in the Chart of Accounts. In the demo company these are 1001 Cash on Hand - Store Registers, 1005 Petty Cash Fund, 1006 Revolving Fund, 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking and 1020 Cash in Bank - BPI Savings.

In the sidebar the module has two groups:

  • Transactions: Write Checks, Cash Voucher, Use Register, Make Deposits, Transfer Funds, Reconcile.
  • Reports: Reconciliation Summary, Reconciliation Detail, Reconciliation Discrepancy, Check Register.

Many other pages also move money: Receive Payments and Enter Sales Receipts bring money in, usually into Undeposited Funds until you deposit it; Pay Bills pays suppliers' bills by check; the POS Register takes cash and card payments. All of them appear in the account registers and in the reconciliation.

Note

The special permission Max check / bill payment / adjustment amount without approval limits the amount a user may pay out on a check or cash voucher on their own. Above it, a supervisor with the approve permission on that page must type their username and password in the Supervisor approval box of the form. User Role Configuration also warns when one role can both enter bills and write checks, receive customer payments and reconcile the bank, or sell at the POS and deposit the cash, because these duties are best kept apart.

Write Checks

Write Checks records a payment made by check, or charged to a company credit card, that is not the payment of a bill already entered: rent paid on the spot, a purchase paid at once, a loan payment, a cash advance by check. To pay bills you have entered, use Pay Bills instead, so the bills are marked paid.

Where to find it: Banking > Write Checks opens the list of checks. The Home page quick action Write Checks opens a blank check. Receipt Management can also open this form filled from a scanned receipt.

The list shows the checks of a date range (from 90 days back to 30 days ahead when it opens). Use From, To, Status, Class and Search (number, name or memo), then click Filter. A voided check is struck through. A check waiting to be printed has a to print tag in the Status column. Click a check number to open it.

Step by step

To write a check:

  1. Open Banking > Write Checks and click New.
  2. Check the Bank / credit card account.
  3. Choose the payee in Pay to the order of, or type an Other payee name.
  4. Check the Date and the Check no.
  5. Choose the Class and type a Memo.
  6. On the Expenses tab, choose the Account, type the Amount and choose the Tax code if the payment includes VAT.
  7. If you are buying items, click the Items tab and enter them.
  8. Check the Total.
  9. If the check is above your limit, open Supervisor approval and let the supervisor type their username and password.
  10. Click Save, or Save & New to write another check.
Example

Check 1006 to Metro Property Leasing for ₱85,000.00, memo "Office and warehouse rent", with one expense line: 6010 Rent Expense, ₱85,000.00.

To print a check voucher:

  1. Open the check.
  2. Click Print.
  3. Click Print above the page.

To print later:

  1. Tick Print later when you write the check.
  2. When you are ready to print, open Banking > Write Checks and click Checks to print. The list shows only the checks marked to be printed.
  3. Open each check and click Print. Once printed, it leaves the list.

To change a check: open it, click Edit, make the change and click Save.

To void or delete a check:

  1. Open the check.
  2. Click Void (keeps the check with no effect on the ledger) or Delete (removes it; a full copy stays in the audit trail).
  3. Type the Reason. If the check is above your amount limit, a supervisor must approve in the same box.
  4. Confirm.

To memorize a check you write every month: open it and click Memorize (see Memorized Transactions).

The saved check shows the date, number, status, payee, the account it was drawn on, the memo and who entered it, then its lines and, for a posted check, General ledger entries created automatically. A voided check has a grey VOID band with the reason and the time.

Buttons

  • New: a blank check.
  • Checks to print: the checks marked Print later that are not printed yet.
  • Export: the list as a spreadsheet file.
  • On a saved check: Edit, Print, Email (when the payee is a supplier or customer; it opens your e-mail program with a link to the check), Memorize, Void, Delete, History (Transaction History) and Back to list.

The check voucher

A check prints as a CHECK VOUCHER with the company name, address and TIN (and the logo when it is switched on for checks in Company, Security & Closing Date), the check number and the date. It shows Pay to with the supplier's or customer's address and TIN, the Amount in figures and in words (for example "Two thousand five hundred pesos and 00/100 only"), the memo, Paid from, the reference, site and class. The ledger entries follow in a table of Account, Memo, Class, Debit and Credit, debits first, with the totals, and the signature lines Prepared by, Checked by, Approved by and Received by. A voided check prints VOID and "No ledger entries (voided)."

What it records

Account Debit Credit
Each expense line's account (for example 6010 Rent Expense) Line amount
The inventory account of each stock item on the Items tab Line amount
1300 Input Tax (or the tax code's input tax account) Tax of the lines
The bank or credit card account of the check (for example 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking) Total of the check

Stock items on the Items tab go into stock at their site at the cost on the line. The class of the check, and the payee, go on every ledger line.

Example

An expense line of ₱10,000.00 with VAT12 posts Dr 6040 Office Supplies Expense ₱10,000.00, Dr 1300 Input Tax ₱1,200.00, Cr 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking ₱11,200.00.

Good to know

  • "Choose a payee or type a payee name."
  • "Choose the bank account."
  • "Add at least one expense or item line."
  • "Choose an account for every expense line." and "Choose an item for every line that has a description or amount."
  • "Supervisor approval is required: check above your amount limit (85,000.00). Ask a supervisor to enter their username and password below." A user who has the approve permission on Write Checks approves their own checks. "You cannot approve your own transaction." appears when a user types their own name in the approval box.
  • When an account on the check is marked With SL, the payee of the check counts as the name of every line, so a check always satisfies the rule when it has a payee from the lists. A typed Other payee name does not count.
  • A check that debits 2000 Accounts Payable lowers the supplier's balance but does not mark any bill paid. Pay entered bills with Pay Bills.
  • Expense lines marked Billable with a Customer:Job are offered as billable costs the next time you create an invoice for that customer (see Create Invoices).
  • Check numbers come from one series shared with the checks of Pay Bills and customer refunds. The program does not stop you from typing a number that was already used; the Check Register highlights duplicates and gaps.
  • A check that has been cleared in a bank reconciliation, or that is dated on or before the closing date, can only be changed by a user with the special permission Can change transactions on/before the closing date or already reconciled. Changes to a cleared check are listed on the Reconciliation Discrepancy report.
  • When you save a check with no class and classes are in use, the program asks No class selected first. Click Choose a class or Continue saving.

Cash Voucher

A cash voucher records cash paid out of a cash fund, such as the petty cash fund or a revolving fund: delivery fares, small purchases, cash advances to employees, government fees. The voucher is entered like a journal entry (the accounts debited and credited), and the cash account is credited for the cash amount. The same page lists the vouchers already saved.

Where to find it: Banking > Cash Voucher. The Cash Voucher button on Employees & Other Names also opens it.

Fields

Field What to enter / what it means
CAV entry no. Leave blank to get the next number (the grey text shows it, for example CAV-00003). A number you type must not be used by another voucher.
Paid to Who received the cash: an employee, customer, supplier or other name. Type part of the name to search.
Cash account The cash fund the money comes out of, for example 1005 Petty Cash Fund. Only Bank accounts are listed.
Transaction date The date of the payment.
or a name that is not on the lists A payee who is on none of the lists. The box is locked while a Paid to name is chosen.
Reference no. An outside reference, for example the receipt number.
Particulars What the cash was for. It prints as the particulars of the voucher.
Cash amount The cash paid out. Leave it blank and it adds up from the lines.

The lines:

Column What to enter / what it means
Account The account charged, for example 6100 Freight and Delivery. Type part of the account name to search.
Debit The amount charged to the account.
Credit Use only when part of the voucher reduces an account, for example a refund of an earlier advance.
Memo The description of the line.
SL The subsidiary ledger name of the line: an employee, customer, supplier or other name. Required on lines to Accounts Receivable (a customer) and Accounts Payable (a supplier). On an account marked With SL, the Paid to name counts when the line has none.

Under the lines, Totals adds the debits and credits, Cash account credited shows the cash amount and Difference shows what is still out of balance. It turns green when the voucher balances.

At the bottom of the form:

Field What to enter / what it means
Class The class of the voucher. It fills the class of the lines and is the class of the cash line.
Reason for the change Shown only when you edit a saved voucher. Recorded in the audit trail.
Show print preview after saving Ticked, the printed voucher opens after Save. The program remembers your choice for the next voucher.

Step by step

To make a cash voucher:

  1. Open Banking > Cash Voucher.
  2. Choose the payee in Paid to, or type the name in or a name that is not on the lists.
  3. Choose the Cash account, for example 1005 Petty Cash Fund.
  4. Check the Transaction date.
  5. Type the Reference no. and the Particulars.
  6. On the first line, choose the Account and type the Debit. Add a Memo and, where needed, the SL name.
  7. Add more lines if the cash was spent on several things.
  8. Leave Cash amount blank, or type the amount on the receipt and check that Difference is 0.00.
  9. Choose the Class.
  10. Click Save, or Save & New to start another voucher. With Show print preview after saving ticked, Save opens the printed voucher.
Example

CAV-00002 pays ₱480.00 of delivery fares out of 1005 Petty Cash Fund to "Jeepney and tricycle fares", with one line: 6100 Freight and Delivery, debit ₱480.00, class Makati Store.

To find a saved voucher:

  1. In Cash voucher list, choose the Date range (Today, This week, This month, Last month, This quarter, This fiscal year, All dates or Custom). For Custom, type the From and To dates; clicking in them switches to Custom.
  2. Choose a Class if you want only one.
  3. Click Query.

To edit, void, delete or print a voucher from the list:

  1. Click any line of the voucher in the list. The footer shows "Selected: CAV-00002".
  2. Click Edit, Void, Delete or Print under the list. Edit and Void are not available for a voided voucher.
  3. For Void or Delete, type the reason and confirm.

Double-click a line, or click its Entry no, to open the voucher.

To clear the form without saving: click Clear.

The saved voucher shows the date, number, status, Paid to, class, Cash account, memo and who entered it, the lines, and General ledger entries created automatically. Its buttons are Edit, Print, Memorize, Void, Delete, History and Back to list.

The voucher list

Column What it shows
Date, Entry no, Paid to Shown on the first line of each voucher.
Account Each account line, then the cash account (greyed).
Subsidiary ledger The SL name of the line; the payee on the cash line.
Memo The memo of the line; the particulars on the cash line.

The footer gives the number of vouchers and the cash paid out by the vouchers that are not void. Export list downloads the list shown as a spreadsheet file.

The printed voucher

The voucher prints as CASH VOUCHER with the company heading, the number and the date; Paid to, Cash account, the Amount in figures and in words, and the Particulars; the class; a table of Account, Description, Name, Class, Debit and Credit that includes the credit to the cash account, with the totals; and the signature lines Prepared by, Checked by, Approved by and Received by, so the payee signs for the cash.

What it records

Account Debit Credit
Each debit line's account (for example 6100 Freight and Delivery) Line amount
Each credit line's account Line amount
The cash account (for example 1005 Petty Cash Fund) Cash amount

Each line carries its SL name and class to the ledger; the cash line carries the payee and the class of the voucher.

Good to know

  • "Enter at least one account line."
  • "Choose the cash account the voucher is paid from."
  • "The cash account is credited automatically for the cash amount. Do not enter it again in the account lines."
  • "Debits less credits (480.00) must equal the cash amount (500.00)." Correct the lines or the cash amount.
  • "The cash amount must be more than zero."
  • "Choose who the cash is paid to, or type the name."
  • "Lines posted to Accounts Receivable need a customer in the SL column." and "Lines posted to Accounts Payable need a supplier in the SL column."
  • "CAV entry no. CAV-00002 is already used. Leave the number blank to get the next one."
  • A voucher above your amount limit needs supervisor approval: "Supervisor approval is required: cash voucher above your amount limit (5,000.00)."
  • When you save with the Class empty and classes are in use, the program asks No class selected first, even when every line has its own class, because the class at the bottom is the class of the cash line.
  • To replenish the fund, move money into it with Transfer Funds or a check payable to the custodian.

Use Register

The register shows every transaction that touched one account, with a running balance, like a checkbook. For a bank account it also lets you record a simple check, deposit or transfer without leaving the page.

Where to find it: Banking > Use Register. The Register link on the Chart of Accounts, and the accounts under Cash and bank accounts on the Home page, open the register of that account.

Step by step

To look at an account's register:

  1. Open Banking > Use Register.
  2. Choose the Account.
  3. Change From and To if needed and click Show.

To enter a simple transaction in the register (bank accounts only):

  1. In Enter a simple transaction in this register, choose the Type: Check / payment, Deposit or Transfer to another account. You see only the types you may create.
  2. Check the Date.
  3. Type the Number: the check number for a check (blank for the next number), or the deposit slip number for a deposit. A transfer does not use it.
  4. Type the Payee / received from name.
  5. Choose the Account (expense / income / transfer to): the account charged by a check, the account credited by a deposit, or the account the money goes to in a transfer.
  6. Type the Amount, choose the Class and type a Memo.
  7. Click Record. The message "Entry recorded." appears and the entry is added to the register.

To export the register: click Export. The file holds the date, type, number, payee, memo, payment, deposit, cleared mark and balance of the lines shown.

Buttons

  • Export: the register as a spreadsheet file.
  • Reconcile: opens Reconcile for this account.

What it records

The register itself records nothing. A quick entry saves an ordinary transaction:

  • Check / payment: a check on Write Checks: Dr the chosen account, Cr this bank account.
  • Deposit: a deposit on Make Deposits: Dr this bank account, Cr the chosen account.
  • Transfer to another account: a transfer on Transfer Funds: Dr the chosen account, Cr this bank account.

You can open, print, edit or void them later on their own pages.

Good to know

  • "Enter the date, a positive amount and the other account."
  • "Quick entry is available for bank and cash accounts only." The quick entry form appears only for Bank accounts.
  • The quick check uses the typed payee name, has one expense line and no tax. For a check with several lines, VAT or a payee from the lists, use Write Checks.
  • The quick entries do not check the With SL rule. For accounts that keep a subsidiary ledger, use Write Checks, Cash Voucher or Make Deposits so the name is recorded.
  • A quick check above your amount limit needs supervisor approval, as on Write Checks.
  • The register shows one line per transaction, even when the transaction posted several lines to the account.

Make Deposits

Make Deposits records money you put in the bank: customer payments and sales receipts waiting in Undeposited Funds, the cash of closed POS shifts, the settlement of card and e-wallet sales by the provider, and other money such as loan proceeds or an owner's contribution. One deposit matches one deposit slip.

Where to find it: Banking > Make Deposits opens a new deposit. The Deposits list button shows the saved deposits. Edit on a saved deposit opens it again to change it (see Edit a deposit).

Fields

Field What to enter / what it means
Deposit to The bank account the money goes into. It starts at 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking.
Date The date of the deposit slip.
Deposit slip no. The number of the bank's deposit slip.
Memo Starts as "Deposit".
Class The class of the deposit.

Payments waiting to be deposited (Undeposited Funds): the customer payments and sales receipts that were put in Undeposited Funds and are not in a deposit yet. Tick the ones on your deposit slip; the box in the heading ticks them all.

Column What it shows
Date, Type, No. The payment or sales receipt. Click the number to open it.
Received from The customer.
Method The payment method, for example Check.
Ref. The check or reference number.
Amount The amount of the payment.

POS cash from closed shifts (shown when there are some): the cash of each closed POS shift not deposited yet. To deposit is the Counted cash, less the opening float (Less float: it stays in the drawer for the next shift), plus the cash drops (Plus drops: cash taken out of the drawer during the shift). Tick the shifts you are depositing. The business date opens the shift, and a shift whose closing entries were corrected says so under its terminal; the figures are always the shift's figures as they are now.

Example

The cashier of POB1 Counter 2 opened with a float of ₱3,000.00 and closed the register with ₱9,170.48 counted, against ₱12,170.48 expected: a shortage of ₱3,000.00. The supervisor finds the drawer held ₱12,170.48 (the cashier had left out the float) and corrects Cash counted to 12,170.48. The shortage entry is voided, and Make Deposits now shows the shift with Counted 12,170.48, Less float 3,000.00 and To deposit 9,170.48: the cash sales of the day, while the ₱3,000.00 float stays in the drawer. Before the correction the shift showed 6,170.48 to deposit.

Card and e-wallet settlements: the provider pays out card and e-wallet sales in one amount, less its fee. The table has one row for each clearing account: the account of every card or e-wallet payment method (Terms, Tax Codes & Other Lists > Payment Methods, POS clearing account) and the module default accounts Card Clearing and E-wallet Clearing, used by a method that names no account. Each account is listed once, with the payment methods that use it under its name, so E-wallet Clearing shared by GCash and Maya is one row carrying the balance of both.

Column What to enter / what it means
Clearing account For example 1060 Card Clearing or 1065 E-wallet Clearing.
Unsettled What the provider has not paid out yet: the balance of the clearing account in the General Ledger on the deposit Date, its debits (card and e-wallet sales and payments) less its credits (settlements and refunds). It is the same figure as the Trial Balance for that date. Changing the Date changes it. A credit balance, in red, means more was settled than was received.
Details Opens the Card and e-wallet balance details of the account (see below): what the unsettled balance is made of.
Gross settled The sales amount the provider is settling. It cannot be more than Unsettled on the deposit date.
Fee deducted The provider's fee taken out of the settlement.
Reference The provider's settlement reference.

Card and e-wallet balance details

Details beside an unsettled balance opens a window that explains it, as of the deposit date. Open as a page shows the same on a page of its own (Banking > Make Deposits, Details), where you can choose another Clearing account or As of date and Print it.

Part What it shows
The balance The unsettled amount, with the payment methods of the account, the total received (debits) and the total settled and refunded (credits). General Ledger and Trial Balance open those reports for the account and date.
What the balance is made of The postings to the account by source: POS sales, POS returns refunded, Customer payments, Sales receipts, Settlements deposited to the bank, journal entries and others, each with the number of Documents, what was Received (debit), what was Settled / refunded (credit) and the Net. The last line, Balance on the date, is the unsettled amount.
Card and e-wallet sales by payment method For each payment method of the account (GCash and Maya on E-wallet Clearing): the POS sales paid with it, the POS refunds, the Payments and sales receipts and the Net received.

Settlements are taken against the oldest receipts first, so the list holds the latest receipts that make up the balance, newest first: Date, Document (click to open it), Customer / terminal, Method, Reference (the approval or reference number typed at the counter), Amount and Unsettled. The oldest one may be partly settled. The total equals the balance.

Example

E-wallet Clearing holds ₱16,113.44: a GCash sale of ₱1,564.64 and a Maya sale of ₱14,548.80. GCash and Maya settle ₱10,000.00. The balance becomes ₱6,113.44, and Receipts not yet settled shows the Maya sale of ₱14,548.80 with ₱6,113.44 unsettled: the ₱10,000.00 paid for the GCash sale and ₱8,435.36 of the Maya sale. The Trial Balance shows ₱6,113.44 on 1065 E-wallet Clearing too.

Step by step

To deposit customer payments:

  1. Open Banking > Make Deposits.
  2. Check Deposit to and the Date.
  3. Type the Deposit slip no.
  4. Tick the payments on the slip under Payments waiting to be deposited.
  5. Check that Deposit total equals the deposit slip.
  6. Click Save deposit, or Save deposit & New to make another one.

To deposit POS cash: tick the shifts under POS cash from closed shifts, then save as above.

To record a card or e-wallet settlement:

  1. Find the clearing account under Card and e-wallet settlements.
  2. Type the Gross settled, the Fee deducted and the provider's Reference.
  3. Save. The bank receives the gross less the fee.

To see what an unsettled balance is made of: click Details beside it. Compare Receipts not yet settled with the provider's statement to find the sales still to be paid out.

Example

GCash settles ₱16,113.44 of e-wallet sales and deducts a fee of ₱322.27. Type 16,113.44 in Gross settled and 322.27 in Fee deducted on 1065 E-wallet Clearing; the deposit total is ₱15,791.17.

To deposit other money: fill a row of Other receipts with Received from, From account, Memo and Amount, then save.

To keep some cash out of the deposit: type the Cash back amount and choose where it goes in Cash back goes to.

To find a saved deposit: click Deposits list. The list works like the other transaction lists: From, To, Status (Posted or Void), Class and Search, then Filter.

To correct a deposit: open it from the list and click Edit (see below). To cancel a whole deposit, click Void, type the reason and confirm: its payments and shifts go back to the waiting lists.

Edit a deposit

Edit on a saved deposit opens Edit Deposit: the same page as Make Deposits, with what the deposit holds already ticked (and shaded), next to the payments and shifts still waiting.

  1. Open the deposit (Banking > Make Deposits > Deposits list, then its number) and click Edit.
  2. Change what is wrong: - untick a payment or a POS shift to take it off the deposit: it goes back to the waiting list, to be deposited another day; - tick another payment or shift to add it; - change the Deposit to account, the Date, the Deposit slip no., the Memo or the Class; - change, add or clear the Other receipts (settlements and fees already on the deposit are listed there too), and the Cash back.
  3. Type the Reason for the change and click Save changes.

The deposit keeps its number and is posted again with the new lines; the change, with the reason and every figure before and after, is in the audit trail. A POS shift always comes in at its figures as they are now ("was ..." under To deposit shows the amount the deposit held for it before). When the closing entries of a deposited shift are corrected later, the deposit follows on its own (see Correcting the closing entries).

  • Editing needs the Edit right on Make Deposits; a voided deposit cannot be edited.
  • A deposit dated on or before the closing date, or one cleared in a bank reconciliation, is protected like every other transaction.
  • "A deposit needs at least one line. To cancel the whole deposit, void it instead."
  • "The cash back (...) is more than the items deposited (...)."

Account Debit Credit
The Deposit to bank account Deposit total

  • "Choose the bank account and deposit date."
  • "Select the payments to deposit or enter other receipts." A deposit needs at least one item.

  • "The fee deducted for Card Clearing is more than the gross settled."

  • "Other receipts need both an account and an amount."
  • A payment that is in a deposit cannot be voided or deleted: "It is included in deposit DEP-00001. Remove it from the deposit first." Take it off the deposit with Edit, or void the deposit.

Transfer Funds

Transfer Funds moves money from one account to another: from the checking account to the petty cash fund, from savings to checking, or a payment of the company credit card from the bank. A transfer does not affect income or expenses.

Where to find it: Banking > Transfer Funds opens a new transfer. The Transfers list button shows the saved transfers.

Fields

Field What to enter / what it means
Date The date of the transfer.
Reference The bank's reference, if any.
Class The class of the transfer.
Transfer funds from The account the money leaves: a Bank, Credit Card or Other Current Asset account.
Transfer funds to The account the money goes into, from the same kinds of account.
Amount The amount transferred.
Memo Starts as "Funds transfer".

Under the form, Current balances shows the balance of every account you can transfer between. It is shown to users who have the special permission View bank balances outside Banking or may open Use Register.

Step by step

To transfer funds:

  1. Open Banking > Transfer Funds.
  2. Check the Date and type the Reference.
  3. Choose Transfer funds from and Transfer funds to.
  4. Type the Amount and change the Memo if needed.
  5. Click Save transfer, or Save transfer & New to make another one. The message says "Transfer recorded. It appears in both registers and does not affect income or expenses."
Example

TRF-00001 moves ₱10,000.00 from 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking to 1005 Petty Cash Fund with the memo "Replenish petty cash".

To change a transfer: open it from Transfers list, click Edit, change it and click Save transfer.

To void or delete a transfer: open it, click Void or Delete, type the reason and confirm.

What it records

Account Debit Credit
Transfer funds to account Amount
Transfer funds from account Amount

The transfer prints as a FUND TRANSFER voucher with From, To, the amount in figures and in words, and the two ledger lines.

Good to know

  • "Choose two different accounts."
  • "Enter an amount greater than zero."
  • Other Current Asset accounts such as 1050 Undeposited Funds and the clearing accounts are listed too. Use Make Deposits for payments and settlements, so each payment is marked deposited.

Reconcile

Reconciling compares your records of a bank account with the bank statement. You tick the checks, payments and deposits that appear on the statement, add the bank's service charge and interest, and finish when the cleared balance equals the statement's ending balance. Reconcile every bank account every month.

Where to find it: Banking > Reconcile, or the Reconcile button on the account's register.

Fields

Field What to enter / what it means
Account The bank account to reconcile. The page reloads when you choose.
Statement date The ending date of the bank statement. It starts at the last day of last month. Only transactions dated on or before it are listed.
Last reconciled The statement date and ending balance of the last completed reconciliation, or "never".
Beginning balance The ending balance of the last completed reconciliation (0.00 the first time). You cannot change it.
Ending balance (from statement) The ending balance printed on the bank statement.
Service charge The bank's service charge on the statement, if any.
Date and Account (service charge) The date of the charge and the expense account. The account starts at 6080 Bank and Merchant Charges.
Class (service charge and interest) The class given to the service charge and interest entries.
Interest earned The interest the bank paid, if any.
Date and Account (interest) The date of the interest and the income account. The account starts at 4200 Interest Income.

Below are two lists of the uncleared transactions: Checks and payments (money out) and Deposits and other credits (money in), each with a Mark all box. Each line shows the ✓ box, Date, No. with the transaction type, Payee and Amount.

At the bottom, Cleared balance is the beginning balance plus the ticked lines, less the service charge, plus the interest. Ending balance repeats the statement's figure and Difference is what is still unexplained: red until it reaches 0.00, then green.

Step by step

To reconcile a bank account:

  1. Open Banking > Reconcile.
  2. Choose the Account.
  3. Set the Statement date to the date of the bank statement.
  4. Type the Ending balance (from statement).
  5. Type the Service charge and the Interest earned shown on the statement, if any, and check their dates and accounts.
  6. Tick each check and payment that appears on the statement.
  7. Tick each deposit and credit that appears on the statement.
  8. Watch Difference. When it is 0.00, click Reconcile now. The message "Reconciliation complete." appears.

To stop and finish later: click Save for later. The ending balance, the ticks, and the service charge and interest with their dates, accounts and class are kept, and the message shows the difference. Nothing is posted yet. When you open Reconcile again for the account, they all come back.

To see the result: in Reconciliation history, click Summary or Detail on the line of the statement.

To undo the last reconciliation:

  1. In Reconciliation history, type the Reason.
  2. Click Undo last and confirm "Undo the last reconciliation?".
  3. The message "The last reconciliation was undone. Its items are uncleared, its service charge and interest entries are voided, and it is open for editing." appears. The reconciliation opens with its ending balance, ticks, service charge and interest as they were. Correct what is wrong and click Reconcile now: the service charge and interest are posted again, once.

If a service charge or interest entry falls in a closed period and you may not change such entries, nothing is undone and the message says why.

Buttons

  • Discrepancy report: opens the Reconciliation Discrepancy report for this account.
  • Save for later: keeps your work without finishing.
  • Reconcile now: finishes the reconciliation. It is enabled only when the difference is 0.00.
  • Undo last: reopens the most recent completed reconciliation. Needs the delete permission on this page.

Reconciliation history

The last twelve reconciliations of the account, with Statement date, Beginning, Ending, Status (Completed or In Progress), By and Reports (Summary and Detail).

What it records

When you click Reconcile now:

Entry Debit Credit
Service Charge (when a service charge is typed) The service charge account (6080 Bank and Merchant Charges) The bank account
Interest Earned (when interest is typed) The bank account The interest account (4200 Interest Income)

Both entries are dated on the dates you gave, numbered in the journal series (JE-) and marked cleared straight away. Every ticked transaction is marked cleared on this account: it shows a ✓ in the register and cleared in its ledger entries, and it no longer appears on the next reconciliation.

Good to know

  • "The difference must be zero to finish. Current difference: 1,500.00." Look for a deposit or check on the statement that you have not ticked, an amount typed differently from the bank, or a transaction not yet entered in the books.
  • "An item changed while you were reconciling. Refresh and try again." Someone changed or deleted a listed transaction while you had the page open.
  • "Enter the statement date."
  • The first reconciliation of an account starts from 0.00, so every transaction up to the statement date is listed, including the opening balance entry. Tick the opening balance too.
  • Save for later keeps the service charge and interest but posts nothing: they are posted only when you click Reconcile now.
  • Undo last voids the service charge and interest entries the reconciliation made (the voided entries stay in the books with the reason, for the audit trail) and keeps their figures on the reopened reconciliation, so finishing it again posts them once.
  • Once cleared, a transaction can only be changed or voided by a user with the special permission Can change transactions on/before the closing date or already reconciled. If its amount on the bank account changes, or it is voided or deleted, the cleared mark is removed and the change is listed on the Reconciliation Discrepancy report.
  • The program asks No class selected only when a service charge or interest is typed and Class (service charge and interest) is empty.
  • Reconciling is best done by someone who does not also receive customer payments.

Banking reports

The Banking reports are listed under Banking > Reports in the sidebar and in the Banking card of the Report Center. The common report tools (Refresh, Print / PDF, Export to spreadsheet, Email link, Modify Report, Memorize and clicking a number to open the transaction) are explained in Working with reports.

Reconciliation Summary

A one-page summary of a completed reconciliation: what was cleared, what was still outstanding, and how the register balance compares with the statement. Print it and file it with the bank statement.

Where to find it: Banking > Reports > Reconciliation Summary, or Summary in the reconciliation history on Reconcile.

Filter: Reconciliation lists every reconciliation as account, statement date and status. Leave it at All to see the most recent completed reconciliation.

How to read it: the heading of the table names the account and the statement date. The lines are:

  • Beginning balance.
  • Cleared transactions: Checks and payments and Deposits and credits, with the number of items and their total (payments as negative figures), then Total cleared transactions and Cleared balance.
  • Uncleared transactions (as of reconciliation): Outstanding checks and payments and Deposits in transit.
  • Register balance as of the statement date: the balance in the books on that date.
  • Statement ending balance.

The note above gives the status, when and by whom it was completed, and a Detail link.

Tip

The register balance equals the statement ending balance plus the deposits in transit, less the outstanding checks. If it does not, a transaction dated on or before the statement date was entered or changed after the reconciliation.

Reconciliation Detail

Lists every transaction of a reconciliation, cleared and uncleared.

Where to find it: Banking > Reports > Reconciliation Detail, or Detail in the reconciliation history.

Filter: Reconciliation, as on the summary.

The columns: Type, Date, Num (click it to open the transaction), Name and Amount, in four groups: Cleared checks and payments, Cleared deposits and credits, Uncleared checks and payments and Uncleared deposits and credits, each with a Total. The note gives the account, statement date and ending balance. A transaction deleted since the reconciliation shows the type "(deleted)".

Reconciliation Discrepancy

Lists the cleared transactions that were changed, voided or deleted after they were reconciled. A clean report means the reconciled balances still stand.

Where to find it: Banking > Reports > Reconciliation Discrepancy, or the Discrepancy report button on Reconcile.

Filter: Account (a bank account, or blank for all).

The columns:

Column What it shows
Changed When the change was made.
Account and Reconciled statement The bank account and the statement date of the reconciliation that cleared it.
Transaction The transaction, for example "Check 1004". Click it to open it.
Change amount changed, voided or deleted.
Cleared amount / New amount The amount on the account when it was cleared, and now.
Effect New amount less cleared amount.
Changed by The user who made the change.

How to use it: check it before each reconciliation. Every line means the last reconciled balance no longer matches the books; ask the user who made the change why, and correct the entry or reconcile the difference.

Check Register

Lists the checks, bill payments and customer refunds in check number order for each account, and highlights missing and duplicate numbers. Use it to account for every check in the checkbook.

Where to find it: Banking > Reports > Check Register

Filters: Dates (starts at This month), From, To and Account (a bank account, or blank for all).

The report can also be narrowed by Vendor and Class (see Filters on related records).

The columns:

Column What it shows
Check no. The number. Click it to open the check.
Date, Account, Type The date, the account code, and Check, Bill Payment or Customer Refund.
Payee and Memo The payee and the memo.
Amount The amount.
Status VOID, to print, printed, or blank.
Exceptions duplicate number, or missing followed by the numbers skipped, for example "missing 1007–1009".

How to read it: lines with an exception are highlighted. A voided check still holds its number, so it is not a gap. A number used on a date outside the range also shows as missing: in the picture, checks 1007 to 1009 were written in August, so widen the dates to the whole year before you look for a lost check.

Note

Checks made out to an employee or other name show a blank Payee on this report. Open the check to see the name.

The Vendors module covers everything you buy and everything you owe: the list of suppliers, purchase orders, receiving the goods, the suppliers' bills, returns and billing corrections (supplier debit memos), advances paid to suppliers before the goods come, and paying the bills, with the taxes withheld from the suppliers. It also keeps the scanned or photographed receipts until they are entered, and it tracks each purchase from order to payment. Everything posts to 2000 Accounts Payable and to the inventory, expense and tax accounts, so the payables reports always agree with the general ledger.

How purchasing works

There are three ways to record a purchase. Choose the one that matches how the supplier delivers and bills.

  • The bill comes with the goods, or it is a service or an expense. Enter it in Enter Bills, or in Receive Items and Enter Bills when the goods were ordered on a purchase order.
  • The goods arrive first and the bill later. Record the delivery in Receive Items. Stock goes up at once. When the bill arrives, turn the receipt into a bill in Enter Bill for Received Items.
  • You pay on the spot. Write a check in Write Checks or a cash voucher in Cash Voucher (see Banking); no bill is needed.

Purchase orders (Create Purchase Orders) are optional. They do not post to the books; they record what was ordered, so you can receive against them and see what is still to come.

Bills are paid in Pay Bills, less the taxes withheld from the supplier when the company is a withholding agent (see BIR Reports). Money paid before the goods or services come is recorded in Advances to Suppliers and applied to the bills later. Returns, price corrections and allowances from a supplier are recorded as Supplier Debit Memos and applied to the supplier's bills. The Bill Tracker shows every purchase at its stage: ordered, received, billed, paid.

The accounts used

Account (demo) Used for
2000 Accounts Payable Every bill, item receipt, debit memo and bill payment
1300 Input Tax The VAT on purchase lines with the VAT12 tax code
1200 Inventory Inventory and assembly items bought, received or returned
6110 Purchases - Non-inventory Non-inventory, service and other charge items with no expense or cost account of their own
5050 Purchase Discounts Early-payment discounts taken in Pay Bills
2110 Withholding Tax Payable The 1% / 2% income tax withheld from suppliers in Pay Bills, owed to the BIR until remitted (0619-E, 1601-EQ)
2115 VAT Withheld Payable The 5% VAT withheld from suppliers, owed to the BIR until remitted (1600-VT)
1410 Advances to Suppliers Money paid to suppliers before their goods or services come, until it is applied to their bills

These come from Module Default Accounts (Accounts Payable, Input Tax, Default Purchases / Expense, Purchase Discounts, Withholding Tax Payable (Expanded), VAT Withheld Payable, Advances to Suppliers), from each tax of the Withholding Tax Table when it has an account of its own, from the tax code's purchase account in Terms, Tax Codes & Other Lists, and from the item's own accounts in the Item List.

Note

Amounts on purchase lines are entered net of VAT. When a line has a tax code, the tax is worked out on the amount and added on top: a ₱10,000.00 expense with VAT12 makes a ₱11,200.00 bill.

The Vendor Center is the list of suppliers with what you owe each one. Select a vendor to see its details, all its transactions and your notes, and to start a purchase order, a bill or a payment for it.

Where to find it: Vendors > Vendor Center.

The left side lists the vendors with their balance. The right side shows the vendor selected (the first one when the page opens).

Good to know

  • The balance is worked out from the Accounts Payable lines of the ledger that carry the vendor: bills and item receipts increase it, bill payments and debit memos decrease it.
  • Vendors cannot be deleted; untick Active instead. An inactive vendor is left out of the Vendor boxes, so make it active again before you edit one of its old documents.
  • Export list downloads the vendors shown (name, company, contact, phone, email, address, TIN, account number and balance) as a CSV file. It needs the print permission.
  • A to-do note added here cannot be marked done from the Vendor Center; it stays in the Home page reminders.
  • To load many vendors at once, use Import from CSV.
  • Giving the same person both the Vendor Center and Pay Bills (creating vendors and paying them) is a combination the administrator is warned about. See System Administration.

What it records

A bill for 10 units of LAP-001 at ₱32,000.00 with VAT12, received at Main Warehouse:

Account Debit Credit
1200 Inventory ₱320,000.00
1300 Input Tax ₱38,400.00
2000 Accounts Payable ₱358,400.00
  • Expense lines debit their own account.
  • Inventory and assembly items debit the item's asset account (1200 Inventory) and add the quantity to the site's stock. The item's average cost is recalculated (weighted average: value on hand divided by quantity on hand).
  • Non-inventory, service and other charge items debit the item's expense account, or its cost of goods sold account, or 6110 Purchases - Non-inventory.
  • The tax of each line is debited to the tax code's purchase account (1300 Input Tax).
  • The total is credited to 2000 Accounts Payable under the vendor.
  • Lines marked Billable wait for the customer's next invoice: they appear under Billable time and costs for this customer in Create Invoices.

Good to know

  • A bill has the status Open until paid, Partial when partly paid, and Paid when its open balance is zero.
  • Editing a bill posts it again: the ledger and the stock are corrected to the new figures.
  • A bill that has payments or debit memos applied cannot be voided or deleted: "Payments or credits have been applied to it. Remove them first (edit the payment or credit)." Edit the bill payment and untick the bill (see Edit a bill payment) or void the payment, or take the debit memo back on its apply page, then void the bill.
  • Messages: "Choose a vendor.", "Add at least one expense or item line.", "Choose an account for every expense line.", "Choose an item for every line that has a description or amount.".
  • Bills dated on or before the closing date can only be entered or changed by users with the special permission for closed periods.
  • Giving the same person Enter Bills and Write Checks is a combination the administrator is warned about. See System Administration.

Applying a debit memo to bills

The apply page shows the Supplier, the Debit memo amount, the amount Already applied and the amount Available to apply. Below are the supplier's Outstanding bills with their Bill amount, Open balance and an Amount to apply box, and the list of Bills this debit memo was applied to.

  1. Type the amount to apply on each bill, or click Apply to the oldest bills first to fill the boxes from the oldest bill down.
  2. Watch the total and the amount "left on the memo". The button is disabled while an amount is more than a bill's open balance or the total is more than the amount available.
  3. Click Apply to the bills.

To undo an application, click Take back beside the bill in Bills this debit memo was applied to and confirm. The amount becomes available on the memo again. To come back to this page later, open the debit memo and click Apply to outstanding bills (or Bills it was applied to once it is fully applied).

The saved debit memo

The saved memo shows its lines, the ledger entries, the Unapplied credit and, under Related documents, the bills it was applied to. The buttons are the same as on a bill, with the apply button in place of Pay Bill.

Print gives the SUPPLIER DEBIT MEMO, to send to the supplier with the returned goods.

  • Applying and taking back need the edit permission on this page.

Pay Bills

Pay Bills pays one or more open bills at once, by check, cash, bank transfer or any other payment method. It creates one bill payment per vendor, takes early-payment discounts, uses the vendor's available debit memos and advances, and deducts the taxes withheld from the supplier (the Withholding column).

Where to find it: Vendors > Pay Bills. It also opens from the Accounts Payable card and the Pay Bills quick action on the Home page, from Pay bills in the Vendor Center, from Pay Bill on a bill, from Pay selected in the Bill Tracker, and from Go to Pay Bills on the Unpaid Bills Detail report.

The page lists all open bills, the oldest due date first. Due dates already past are shown in red.

Choose a Vendor and click Filter to see only that vendor's bills.

Step by step: pay bills

  1. Go to Vendors > Pay Bills. Filter by Vendor or Due on or before if you like.
  2. Tick the bills to pay. Change Amount to pay for a partial payment.
  3. Where a discount is available (avail. under the Discount box), ticking the bill fills it in and the amount to pay leaves it out.
  4. Where the vendor has credits or advances, type the part to use in Use credit or Use advance: the amount to pay goes down by the same figure.
  5. Set the Payment date, the Method and the Payment account. Type the Reference for a transfer.
  6. Tick Queue checks for printing if you will print the checks later.
  7. Click Pay selected bills. With one vendor, the payment opens; with several, the list of bill payments opens.
Example

Industrial Machines Corp. has three open bills. Tick IMC-SV-0192 (₱12,500.00), leave the amount, choose Method Check and Payment account 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking, and click Pay selected bills. The program records one bill payment of ₱12,500.00, numbered in the check series, and the bill becomes Paid.

The Withholding column

When the company is a withholding agent, each bill has a Withholding box. Ticking the bill ticks the supplier's usual taxes (its Withholding taxes in the Vendor Center) and works out their amounts at once; the box shows the total withheld and the rates used (1%, or 1% + 5%), and Amount to pay drops by the same figure.

Click the box to open the list of the Withholding Tax Table:

The base and the amounts are there even before the bill is ticked: open the Withholding box of any bill and the Tax base, net of VAT already shows its outstanding balance net of VAT (less the early-payment discount, when there is one). Ticking a tax works out its amount at once, ticks the bill and lowers Amount to pay. The amount can still be typed over for an adjustment.

Part What it shows / what to do
Tax base, net of VAT The base the taxes are worked out on: what the line settles (the open balance less the discount and the credit) without its VAT. For a bill of ₱112,000.00 with 12% VAT it is ₱100,000.00. It follows the line until you type a base of your own.
A tick box per tax Tick one or more taxes to withhold on this bill (one or more per invoice paid). Each shows its name, rate and ATC, for example WT on Goods 1%, ATC WC158. A tax set up on the gross amount says so.
The amount of each tax Worked out when the tax is ticked: the base times the rate (₱100,000.00 × 1% = ₱1,000.00). Type over it to adjust; a typed amount is kept when the base changes.
Total withheld The sum of the ticked taxes: the figure the Withholding box shows
Clear, Done Clear unticks every tax of the bill; Done closes the list
Step by step: pay a bill less the taxes withheld
  1. Go to Vendors > Pay Bills and choose the Vendor.
  2. Tick the bill. The supplier's usual taxes are ticked and the amount to pay is already less the tax.
  3. To change the taxes, click the Withholding box: tick or untick taxes, or type over an amount, then click Done. You can also start here: ticking a tax on a bill that is not ticked yet ticks the bill, with the amount worked out from its outstanding balance.
  4. Set the Payment date, Method and Payment account and click Pay selected bills.
  5. Print the supplier's certificate at the end of the quarter with Print BIR Form 2307 (or from the BIR Form 2307 button of the payment).

Example (the company guide's case D): a supplier bills ₱112,000.00 for goods: ₱100,000.00 plus ₱12,000.00 VAT. The company withholds 1% income tax (WT on Goods) and 5% VAT (WT on VAT), both on the ₱100,000.00 net of VAT: ₱1,000.00 and ₱5,000.00. Amount to pay becomes ₱106,000.00. The payment debits Accounts Payable ₱112,000.00 and credits the bank ₱106,000.00, Withholding Tax Payable ₱1,000.00 and VAT Withheld Payable ₱5,000.00, and the bill is Paid.

The saved payment lists the taxes in Taxes withheld from the supplier (the bill, the tax with its ATC, the base and the amount) and has a BIR Form 2307 button that opens the certificate of the supplier for the quarter of the payment.

Using an advance

When a vendor has advances left (see Advances to Suppliers), Pay Bills shows the Use advance column, with what is left of the vendor's advances under each box. Type the part of the advance to apply in Use advance: the amount to pay drops by it. On saving, the advance is applied first (oldest advance first) as a payment of its own, numbered AAP-00001 and so on, that debits Accounts Payable and credits Advances to Suppliers; what is left is paid from the payment account. The taxes withheld and the discount of the bill go with the cash payment, or with the advance when the bill is settled out of the advance alone.

The list of bill payments

Bill payments at the top of Pay Bills opens the list of payments made, with the usual date, class and search filters. Its Status filter offers To be printed (payments queued for printing) and Void. Click a number to open a payment; Pay bills returns to Pay Bills.

An open bill payment shows its details, the bills it paid, and the ledger entries. The buttons are Edit, Print, Email, Void, Delete, History and Back to list. Edit appears for users with the edit permission on Pay Bills.

Step by step: change a bill payment
  1. Open the bill payment (Vendors > Pay Bills > Bill payments, then its number) and click Edit.
  2. Change what is wrong: the date, the account paid from, the check number, or the amounts on the bills. Untick a bill the payment should not pay; tick a bill it should pay.
  3. Type the Reason for the change.
  4. Click Save changes. The payment opens with the new figures.
Example

Bill payment 1008 to TechDistributors Inc. paid ₱1,127,280.00 on TD-INV-10231 from 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking, but the money left the BPI account. Click Edit, choose Payment account 1020 Cash in Bank - BPI Savings, type the reason "paid from BPI" and click Save changes. The ledger now credits 1020 instead of 1010; the bill stays as it was. To pay TD-INV-10288 with the same check as well, tick it before saving: its ₱75,040.00 is filled in and the check becomes ₱1,202,320.00.

What happens when you save:

  • The payment is posted again: Accounts Payable is debited and the payment account credited for the new total (and Purchase Discounts for the new discounts, and the withholding tax payable accounts for the new taxes withheld).
  • A payment that applies an advance (AAP-...) is not edited: void it and apply the advance again in Pay Bills.
  • Every bill the payment paid before or pays now gets its balance and status worked out again (Open, Partial or Paid).
  • The vendor stays the same. A payment made to the wrong vendor is voided and entered again.
  • Vendor credits used on the bills are not part of the payment; they stay as they are (take them back on the debit memo's apply page).
  • A new total above your amount limit needs supervisor approval, as on Pay Bills.
  • A printed check whose amount, number or date changed shows a reminder to print it again.
  • Payments dated on or before the closing date, and payments cleared in a bank reconciliation, are protected as every other transaction is.
  • Messages: "Tick at least one bill and enter the amount paid on it. To cancel the whole payment, void it instead.", "Payment for ... is more than what is left on it (...)", "Check / payment number ... is already used by another check or bill payment.", "A voided bill payment cannot be edited.".

Step by step: print the checks

  1. Open the bill payment (for queued checks, open Bill payments and choose Status To be printed).
  2. Click Print. The voucher opens: CHECK VOUCHER for a check payment, PAYMENT VOUCHER for other methods. It shows the payee with address and TIN, the amount in figures and in words, the account paid from, the bills paid with their discounts, the ledger entries, and Prepared by, Checked by, Approved by and Received by lines.
  3. Click Print on the voucher page.

Opening the print marks the payment as printed and removes it from To be printed.

What it records

One payment per vendor, with the vendor's bills listed on it:

Account Debit Credit
2000 Accounts Payable Amount paid plus discount
The Payment account, for example 1010 Cash in Bank - BDO Checking Amount paid
5050 Purchase Discounts Discount
2110 Withholding Tax Payable (or the tax's own account) Income tax withheld (1% / 2%)
2115 VAT Withheld Payable (or the tax's own account) VAT withheld (5%)

Accounts Payable is debited with everything the bill is settled by: the cash, the discount and the taxes withheld. An advance applied is a payment of its own: Dr 2000 Accounts Payable, Cr 1410 Advances to Suppliers.

Each bill's open balance goes down by the amount paid, the discount and the taxes withheld, and its status becomes Partial or Paid. Credits used are applied from the vendor's debit memos (oldest first) without a ledger entry. When a vendor's bills are settled with credits only, no payment document is made for that vendor.

Good to know

  • Opening Pay Bills needs the create permission on it; the list of bill payments needs only access to the page; Edit on a bill payment needs the edit permission.
  • When the largest payment to one vendor is more than your amount limit (Max check / bill payment / adjustment amount without approval), a supervisor must type their username and password in Supervisor approval, unless you have the Approve permission on Pay Bills. Voiding or deleting a payment above your limit needs approval too.
  • Voiding a bill payment reopens the bills it paid. Credits used from debit memos stay applied; take them back on the debit memo's apply page if needed.
  • Bill payments share the check number series with Write Checks (1008, 1009 ...).
  • The Withholding column is shown only when This company is a withholding agent is on (Withholding Tax Table). The taxes are kept by bill and type, which is what BIR Form 2307 and the BIR Reports are made from.
  • Messages: "Select at least one bill and enter the amount to pay.", "Payment for ... is more than its open balance of ..." (the amount paid, discount, credit, advance and taxes withheld together), "A withholding tax ticked on ... is not on the Withholding Tax Table (or is inactive).", "Only ... of advances is left for ...", "Not enough vendor credit available for ...", "Amounts cannot be negative.", "Choose the account to pay from.", "Enter the payment date."
  • The early-payment discount shown is worked out for today's date when the page opens.

The filters: Dates (this month by default), From, To, Vendor and Type (one kind of transaction).

The report can also be narrowed by Class (see Filters on related records).

The columns: Type ("(void)" is added to voided ones), Date, Num, Vendor ref., Memo, Account (the bank account for payments and checks, Accounts Payable for bills), Clr (a check mark when cleared in a bank reconciliation) and Amount (payments, checks and debit memos negative). Purchase orders are listed for reference; they do not post.

Vendor Contact List

The active vendors with their contact details and the balance owed.

Where to find it: Vendors > Reports > Vendor Contact List.

The filters: Search (vendor name or contact person).

The columns: Vendor (click to open it in the Vendor Center), Contact, Phone, Fax, Email, Billing address, Account no. (your account number with the vendor) and Balance owed. TIN and Withholding taxes (the supplier's usual taxes of the Withholding Tax Table) can be added with Modify Report.

Expenses by Vendor Summary

How much was spent with each supplier in a period: everything charged to the expense accounts (expenses, cost of goods sold and other expenses) by bills, checks, cash vouchers, card charges and journal entries that name the supplier, less supplier debit memos.

Where to find it: Vendors > Reports > Expenses by Vendor Summary.

The filters: Dates (this month as delivered), From, To, Columns (Total only, or one column per Month, Quarter or Year, starting with the first period that has an expense) and Class.

The report can also be narrowed by Vendor and Expense account (see Filters on related records).

The columns: Vendor (click for the detail), Transactions, the period columns if chosen, Total expenses and % of total.

Note

Purchases of inventory items go to the inventory account and become cost of goods sold only when they are sold, so a bill for stock is not an expense here. Expenses that name no supplier (a petty-cash payment to an employee, depreciation) are not listed either. The total agrees with the expense accounts of the General Ledger for the lines that name a supplier.

Expenses by Vendor Detail

Every expense charged to each supplier in the period, with the account it went to.

Where to find it: Vendors > Reports > Expenses by Vendor Detail.

The filters: Dates, From, To, Vendor, Class and Expense account (one of the expense, cost of goods sold and other expense accounts).

The columns: for each supplier a heading line, then Type, Date, Num (the supplier's reference, or the number; click to open the transaction), Memo, Account (click for its General Ledger), Class, Amount and Balance (running within the supplier), and a total per supplier. Supplier debit memos are negative amounts.

Advances to Suppliers

What is left of the money paid to suppliers in advance, supplier by supplier, as of a date.

Where to find it: Vendors > Reports > Advances to Suppliers, or Advances report on the advance form.

The filters: As of (today as delivered), Vendor, and Show (With something left to apply, or Every advance).

The columns: for each supplier a heading line, then Date paid, Advance no. (click to open it), What it pays for, Days outstanding, Advance, Applied to bills and Left to apply, with a total per supplier. Check / ref. and Expected can be added with Modify Report.

Under the report, the balance of 1410 Advances to Suppliers in the general ledger as of the same date is shown; when it differs from the advances left (an entry made to the account directly, for example), the difference is given.

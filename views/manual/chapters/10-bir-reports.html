The BIR Reports module holds what GSMaRT Construction System needs for the company's withholding tax and VAT returns: the Withholding Tax Table that says how much is withheld, the BIR Form 2307 certificate given to each supplier, and the reports from which the returns are prepared (2550Q, 1600-VT, 0619-E and 1601-EQ). The taxes themselves are withheld where the payments are made: in Pay Bills for what the company withholds from its suppliers, and in Receive Payments for what customers withhold from the company.

The rules and the worksheets follow the company's guide VAT, Withholding Tax and Company Tax Returns (prepared 08 October 2026).

In the sidebar the module has three groups:

  • Transactions: Print BIR Form 2307.
  • Setup: Withholding Tax Table.
  • Reports, under the heading BIR Reports: Summary of Taxes Withheld from Vendors/Suppliers, Expanded Withholding Tax Register (1% / 2%), Withholding Tax Remittances (0619-E, 1601-EQ, 1600-VT), VAT Withholding Register (1600-VT), Output VAT / Sales Register, Input VAT / Purchases Register, Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule (2550Q Schedule 3) and Quarterly VAT Return Worksheet (2550Q). They are also listed in the Report Center under BIR Reports.

The taxes at a glance

Tax Who handles it Usual rate Where it goes
Output VAT The VAT-registered seller 12% of the VAT-exclusive taxable sale BIR 2550Q: output VAT
Input VAT The eligible VAT-registered buyer Normally 12% of the eligible VAT-exclusive purchase BIR 2550Q: allowable input VAT
VAT withheld A qualifying government buyer / withholding agent Generally 5% of the VAT-exclusive payment Buyer: 1600-VT. Seller: a VAT credit on 2550Q (Item 16)
Creditable withholding on goods Government buyers and BIR-notified top withholding agents Generally 1% of the income payment Buyer: 0619-E / 1601-EQ. Seller: an income-tax credit
Creditable withholding on services Government buyers and BIR-notified top withholding agents Generally 2% of the income payment Buyer: 0619-E / 1601-EQ. Seller: an income-tax credit
Important

The 5% VAT withholding is not the VAT rate, and it is worked out on the amount net of VAT, never on the VAT itself. For a ₱112,000.00 invoice (₱100,000.00 plus ₱12,000.00 VAT) the 5% is ₱5,000.00 and the 1% ₱1,000.00; the supplier receives ₱106,000.00. The 1% / 2% are income tax, not VAT: they never reduce the VAT due and are never posted as input VAT.

Who must withhold: government payors and the top withholding agents notified by the BIR withhold 1% on goods and 2% on services bought from their regular suppliers (RR 11-2018); government buyers also withhold 5% VAT on their qualifying VATable purchases. An ordinary private purchaser generally withholds neither. Check the thresholds (single purchases of ₱10,000.00 or less), the supplier's status and exemptions, and the special rates (0.5% under RR 24-2025, the rules of RR 9-2025 for registered business enterprises) before you withhold.

They are set in Module Default Accounts (Withholding Tax Payable (Expanded), VAT Withheld Payable, Creditable Withholding Tax, Creditable VAT Withheld). A tax of the Withholding Tax Table can have accounts of its own instead.

Withholding Tax Table

The Withholding Tax Table is the guide to how much is deducted: every tax that can be withheld from a payment, with its rate and the base it is worked out on. It is flexible: taxes can be added, changed, made inactive or deleted at any time. The same page says whether the company is a withholding agent and holds the payor details printed on BIR Form 2307.

Where to find it: BIR Reports > Withholding Tax Table. Viewing it needs access to the page; adding, changing and deleting need the create, edit and delete permissions (by default the Administrator and the Accountant).

The withholding agent card

Field What it means
This company is a withholding agent On: the company deducts withholding taxes when it pays its suppliers. Pay Bills shows the Withholding column and BIR Form 2307 is printed for the suppliers. Off: Pay Bills has no Withholding column (a payment that already withheld taxes keeps them when it is edited). On as delivered.
Payor on BIR Form 2307 The company name, TIN and address of System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date, printed in Part II of the form
Payor zip code Printed in Item 8A of the form
Authorized signatory, Title / designation, Signatory TIN Printed over the signature line of the payor on the form

Click Save to keep them; the change goes to the audit trail.

The table

Column What it shows
Type The name of the tax, its code and when it applies
ATC The BIR alphanumeric tax code printed on Form 2307 (for example WC158)
Rate The percentage withheld
Kind Income tax withheld (the expanded withholding tax, 1% / 2%) or VAT withheld (the business tax, 5%). The kinds are kept apart in every report and on the form.
Worked out on Amount net of VAT (as the BIR asks for these rates) or Gross amount (VAT included)
Used on Payments to suppliers, Payments from customers or Both
Accounts Where it posts: what is withheld from suppliers, and what customers withhold
Used How many payment lines it was withheld on, and how many suppliers have it as a usual tax
Status Active, or Inactive (no longer offered on new payments)

As delivered, the table holds:

Type (code) ATC Rate Kind
WT on Goods (WT-GOODS) WC158 1% Income tax withheld
WT on Services (WT-SERVICES) WC160 2% Income tax withheld
WT on VAT (WT-VAT) WV010 5% VAT withheld

All three are worked out on the amount net of VAT and used on both sides. Check the ATCs against the BIR's current list for your company (corporations and individuals have different codes).

Step by step: add a tax

  1. Click Add a withholding tax.
  2. Type the Name, a short Code of your own (unique), the BIR ATC and the Rate (%) (0.5 for 0.5%).
  3. Choose the Kind of tax, what it is Worked out on and where it is Used on.
  4. Leave the two account boxes on their default, or choose an account of its own.
  5. Type When it applies, the Order on the list, and click Save.

Changing, deactivating and deleting

  • Edit changes a tax. Payments already saved keep the rate and amount they were saved with; the new rate applies to payments from then on.
  • Untick Active to stop offering a tax. A payment that used it still shows it.
  • The bin button deletes a tax that has never been withheld on a payment (it is also taken off the suppliers and customers that had it as a usual tax). A tax already used stays on the table: "... has been used on ... payment line(s), so it stays on the table. Make it inactive instead."
  • Messages: "Enter the code and the name of the withholding tax.", "The rate is a percentage above 0 and up to 100 (1 for 1%).", "Another withholding tax already has the code ...".

How the table is used

  • Vendor Center: each supplier's usual taxes (Withholding taxes on the vendor form), one, two or more.
  • Pay Bills: the Withholding column opens this table for each bill; tick one or more taxes (for example 1% and 5%). Each amount is worked out at once from the outstanding balance net of VAT and can be typed over. See The Withholding column.
  • Receive Payments: the taxes a customer withheld are ticked the same way.
  • Each payment keeps its taxes by bill (or invoice) and type, with the base, rate and ATC. BIR Form 2307 and the reports below are made from them.

Print BIR Form 2307

BIR Form 2307, the Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source, is given by the company to each supplier it withheld taxes from, for each quarter. GSMaRT Construction System prints it from the taxes withheld in Pay Bills in the quarter.

Where to find it: BIR Reports > Print BIR Form 2307. The BIR Form 2307 button of a bill payment with taxes withheld opens it for that supplier and quarter. Printing needs the print permission on the page.

The page

Choose the Supplier, the Year and the Quarter, and click Show. On the left, Suppliers with taxes withheld lists every supplier the company withheld from in the quarter, with its TIN, the income tax and VAT withheld, and whether its 2307 has been printed (Printed and the date, or Not yet printed). Click a supplier to see its certificate.

A warning shows when the supplier or the company has no TIN.

What the certificate holds

The layout follows the official form (January 2018 ENCS):

Part What is printed
Item 1 For the Period From ... To ...: the first and last day of the quarter
Part I - Payee Information Item 2 the supplier's TIN, Item 3 its registered name (the company name of the vendor, or its name), Item 4 its address
Part II - Payor Information Item 6 the company's TIN, Item 7 its name, Item 8 its address, Item 8A the payor zip code
Part III - Details of Monthly Income Payments and Taxes Withheld One line per tax with its ATC: the income payments (the tax bases, net of VAT) of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Month of the Quarter, their Total and the Tax Withheld for the Quarter; then Money Payments Subject to Withholding of Business Tax (Government & Private) for the VAT withheld, each with a total
Declaration and signatures The declaration of the form, the authorized signatory of the payor (from the Withholding Tax Table), and the CONFORME line for the payee

The month of a payment is the month of the bill payment's date.

Step by step: print the certificates of a quarter

  1. Go to BIR Reports > Print BIR Form 2307 and choose the Year and Quarter.
  2. To print one supplier's certificate, click it in the list, check the figures and click Print / PDF, then Print / Save as PDF on the print page.
  3. To print every supplier's certificate at once, click Print all: one certificate per page.
  4. Sign the certificates and give them to the suppliers.

Each print is recorded in the audit trail (action print_2307), which is how the list and the Expanded Withholding Tax Register know that a supplier's 2307 was issued.

Example

in the demo, the bookkeeping fee of Accounting Partners & Co. (APC-2231, ₱45,000.00 plus ₱5,400.00 VAT) was paid less 2% withheld on the ₱45,000.00: ₱900.00. Its 2307 shows one line, WT on Services 2%, ATC WC160, ₱45,000.00 in the month it was paid, and ₱900.00 withheld.

Good to know

  • Only payments that are not void count. A payment voided after the certificate was printed changes the certificate: print it again.
  • The certificate uses the bases and amounts saved on the payments: correct a wrong amount by editing the bill payment.
  • The amounts customers withheld from the company are on the 2307 forms they give you; record them in Receive Payments and follow them with the Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule and the Expanded Withholding Tax Register (taxes withheld by customers).

BIR Reports

The reports follow the worksheets of the guide (Forms A to F). Each one prints, exports to a spreadsheet, memorizes and can be redesigned like every report (see Home, Finding Things and Working with Reports), and ends with Prepared by, Reviewed by and Date lines and the note Internal worksheet only, not a substitute for BIR filing forms. File the official BIR forms (through eFPS or eBIRForms) from them, and check the current BIR issuances and form versions first.

The Dates box of every report has This quarter and Last quarter among its choices. The reports open on this quarter, the Remittances on this year and the VAT Withholding Register on this month.

Summary of Taxes Withheld from Vendors/Suppliers

What the company withheld from each supplier on the bills paid in the period, by supplier and tax: the summary asked for by the company, and the figures of the quarterly alphalist of payees attached to 1601-EQ.

The filters: Dates, From, To, Vendor, Tax (income tax withheld or VAT withheld) and Withholding tax (one type of the table).

The columns: Supplier (click it to open its BIR Form 2307 for the quarter), TIN, ATC, Withholding tax, Rate, Income payment (tax base) and Tax withheld, with a total per supplier. Kind and Payments can be added. Under the report, the income tax withheld and the VAT withheld are given apart.

Expanded Withholding Tax Register (1% / 2%)

Form E of the guide: every 1% / 2% income tax withheld, payment by payment.

The filters: Dates, From, To, Vendor, Customer and Register of: Taxes withheld from suppliers (the payments the company withholds from; reconcile with 0619-E and 1601-EQ) or Taxes withheld by customers (the income-tax credits to support with the customers' 2307 forms).

The columns: Date paid, Payee (supplier) or Payor (customer), TIN, Invoice / reference (click to open the bill or invoice), Goods / services (the tax), VAT-exclusive income base, EWT rate, Income tax withheld, 2307 status (for suppliers: Printed and the date, or Not yet printed; for customers: ask the customer for it) and Remittance / return (0619-E due the 10th of the next month for months 1 and 2 of the quarter, 1601-EQ due the last day of the month after the quarter for month 3). ATC and Payment no. can be added.

Withholding Tax Remittances (0619-E, 1601-EQ, 1600-VT)

What the company withheld from its suppliers and must remit, return by return, with the due dates of the guide's filing table.

The filters: Dates (this fiscal year as delivered), From and To.

The columns: Return, Period, Due date, ATC, Withholding tax, Rate, Tax base and Tax withheld, with a total per return and period.

  • 0619-E: the income tax withheld in months 1 and 2 of a quarter, monthly, by the 10th of the following month.
  • 1601-EQ: the quarter's expanded withholding return, by the last day of the month after the quarter. It lists the income tax withheld in the third month, and the quarter's 0619-E remittances are shown as a credit under it.
  • 1600-VT: the VAT withheld, monthly, by the 10th of the following month.
Note

eFPS filers and taxpayers covered by special issuances can have other dates. Always check the BIR tax calendar before filing. The annual 1604-E and its alphalist are prepared from the Summary of Taxes Withheld over the year.

VAT Withholding Register (1600-VT)

Form F of the guide: the 5% VAT withheld from suppliers, for 1600-VT and the certificates.

The filters: Dates (this month as delivered), From, To and Vendor.

The columns: Date paid, VAT-registered seller, TIN, Invoice reference, VAT-exclusive payment, VAT WHT rate, VAT withheld (1600-VT), 1600-VT due, Income EWT 1% / 2% (withheld on the same bill and payment; it goes on 0619-E / 1601-EQ) and BIR 2307 issued.

Note

As the guide warns, this register is for the government 5% VAT withholding. Do not use it for an ordinary private buyer without an applicable special rule; for a registered business enterprise review the special 12% rules first.

Output VAT / Sales Register

Form B of the guide: the taxable sales reported in full, even when customers withhold taxes from the payment.

The filters: Dates, From, To and Customer. The Columns box picks the columns to show.

The columns: Date, VAT invoice no. (click to open it), Customer, TIN, Goods / services, Net taxable sales, Output VAT 12%, Invoice total (inc. VAT), Income WHT 1% / 2%, VAT WHT 5% (the taxes the customers withheld on it) and Actual cash received. Type, Zero-rated sales and Exempt sales can be added.

Invoices, sales receipts, statement charges and POS sales are listed; credit memos and POS returns are negatives. A line is VATable when its tax code has a rate (VAT12), zero-rated with the ZR code, and exempt otherwise. Reconcile Net taxable sales and Output VAT 12% to 2550Q Items 31A and 31B.

Input VAT / Purchases Register

Form C of the guide: the purchases and their input VAT.

The filters: Dates, From, To and Vendor. The Columns box picks the columns to show.

The columns: Date, VAT invoice no. (the supplier's reference), Supplier, TIN, Category (Goods, Services or Capital goods, from the lines), VAT-exclusive purchase, Input VAT, Exempt / non-VAT purchases, Total bill (inc. VAT), Income WHT, Amount paid to supplier and VAT status. Type, Zero-rated purchases and VAT WHT can be added.

Bills and checks are listed; supplier debit memos are negatives. Item receipts are not (the VAT is recorded when the bill is entered).

Note

Input VAT is claimable only where the company meets the invoicing and creditability requirements. A non-VAT supplier's invoice has no claimable input VAT. Identify noncreditable, exempt, import, capital-goods, unpaid-payable and adjustment amounts separately.

Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule (2550Q Schedule 3)

Form D of the guide: the 5% VAT government customers withheld from the company, the VAT credit of 2550Q Part V Schedule 3 and Item 16.

The filters: Dates, From, To and Customer.

The columns: Payment date, Government withholding agent, TIN, VAT invoice reference, Withholding tax, VAT-exclusive tax base, VAT WHT rate and VAT amount withheld. Quarter and Payment no. can be added.

Match each line to the government purchaser, the invoice, the proof of payment and the BIR Form 2307 received, and tick it off once the certificate is in hand. Government VAT withheld is not input VAT.

Quarterly VAT Return Worksheet (2550Q)

Form A of the guide, the quarterly VAT reconciliation, with the item numbers of BIR Form 2550Q (April 2024 edition).

The filters: Dates (this quarter as delivered), From and To. Run it for one quarter.

Item What it is Where it comes from
31A, 31B VATable sales net of VAT, and their output VAT The Output VAT / Sales Register
32, 33, 34A Zero-rated sales, exempt sales, total sales The same register
37B Total adjusted output VAT 31B, with no adjustments
38 Input VAT carried over from the previous quarter The excess input VAT of the quarter before (when its allowable input VAT was more than its output VAT)
44A, 44B Domestic purchases net of VAT, and their input tax The Input VAT / Purchases Register
48A Domestic purchases with no input tax Zero-rated, exempt and non-VAT purchases
50B, 51B, 60B Current, total available and total allowable input tax 44B, plus 38
61 Net VAT payable before payment credits 37B less 60B
15 Net VAT payable carried to the tax payable section 61 when it is positive
16 Creditable VAT withheld (Schedule 3) The Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule
17, 20 Advance VAT payments / other credits, total credits 16 (no other credits are kept)
21 Tax still payable, or the excess input VAT carried to the next quarter 15 less 20

The last line, under NOT VAT, gives the 1% / 2% income tax withheld by customers: an income-tax credit, kept apart because it never reduces the VAT due.

Example (the guide's sample quarter): VATable sales of ₱350,000.00 with output VAT ₱42,000.00 (Items 31A, 31B, 37B); purchases of ₱100,000.00 with input VAT ₱12,000.00 (44A, 44B, 50B, 51B, 60B); net VAT before credits ₱30,000.00 (61, 15); creditable VAT withheld by government buyers ₱10,000.00 (16, 20); VAT still payable ₱20,000.00 (21). The ₱5,000.00 of income tax withheld on the sales is shown apart and does not reduce the ₱20,000.00.

2550Q is filed quarterly, generally within 25 days after the quarter closes. The official form has more items (uncollected receivables, capital goods, importations, special adjustments) than this worksheet: use the actual amounts and the latest BIR instructions.

A company's monthly-to-quarterly routine

  1. Keep each supplier's and customer's tax profile current: VAT status, TIN, whether it is a government or top withholding agent, its usual withholding taxes.
  2. Enter the VAT invoices with the VATable, zero-rated and exempt amounts and the tax codes.
  3. Withhold the taxes as separate taxes in Pay Bills (and record what customers withheld in Receive Payments). Never net the 5% VAT withheld and the 1% / 2% against the same VAT account.
  4. Each month: run the Withholding Tax Remittances for 0619-E and 1600-VT.
  5. Each quarter: print the BIR Form 2307 certificates, run the Summary of Taxes Withheld for 1601-EQ, and the 2550Q worksheet with its registers for the VAT return.
  6. Reconcile the registers with the general ledger: 2100 Output Tax, 1300 Input Tax, 2110, 2115, 1310 and 1315, and keep the acknowledgments of the returns filed.

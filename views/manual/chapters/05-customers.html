This chapter covers the work you do for the people and companies you sell to: keeping customer records, quoting prices, taking orders, billing, recording cash sales, collecting payments, giving credits and refunds, sending statements and charging interest on overdue accounts. The last part explains the customer, receivables and sales reports.

All the pages are under Customers in the sidebar. What you see and what you can do on each page depends on your role in Page Access Configuration: view, create, edit, delete, print and approve. If a button described here is missing on your screen, your role does not allow that action (see System Administration).

How a sale moves through the program

A sale can start as a quotation and end as money in the bank. You do not have to use every step: a walk-in sale goes straight to a sales receipt, and a regular account customer can be invoiced without an estimate or an order.

Step Page Effect on the books
Quote a price Create Estimates None
Take an order Create Sales Orders None
Bill on account Create Invoices Debits Accounts Receivable
Bill many customers at once Create Batch Invoices Debits Accounts Receivable
Sell for cash Enter Sales Receipts Debits Undeposited Funds
Add a charge to an account Enter Statement Charges Debits Accounts Receivable
Charge interest on late accounts Assess Finance Charges Debits Accounts Receivable
Collect Receive Payments Credits Accounts Receivable
Take goods back or give a credit Create Credit Memos/Refunds Credits Accounts Receivable
Bank the collections Banking > Make Deposits Moves Undeposited Funds to the bank
Remind the customer Create Statements None

Estimates and sales orders are non-posting documents: they record a commitment but never touch the ledger or the stock on hand. Everything else in the table posts to the general ledger the moment you save it (unless you save an invoice or sales receipt as pending, explained under Create Invoices).

Collections are recorded in 1050 Undeposited Funds by default. They stay there until you group them into a bank deposit with Make Deposits, exactly as the cashier brings the checks and cash to the bank.

Customers and jobs

A job is a record kept under a customer, for a project or a branch you bill separately. It is shown as Customer:Job, for example ABC Trading Corp.:Office Renovation Project. A job has its own address, terms, price level and transactions, and it rolls up into its customer:

  • The customer's open balance includes the balances of its jobs.
  • Receive Payments is done for the customer; the invoices of its jobs are listed with it.
  • Statements are made per customer and include the jobs.
  • Finance charges are assessed on the customer, for the overdue documents of the customer and its jobs.
  • The credit limit that counts is the customer's own, compared with the balance of the customer and all its jobs.

A job cannot have jobs of its own.

Lists to set up first

Before you enter customers, make sure these lists hold what you need. They are kept under Terms, Tax Codes & Other Lists in the Financial Set-up & Reports module:

  • Payment terms: name, net days and an optional early-payment discount (for example "2% 10 Net 30": 2% off if paid within 10 days, due in 30).
  • Tax codes: for example VAT12 (12%), ZR, EX and NT, each with its output tax account.
  • Payment methods: Cash, Check, GCash, Bank Transfer and so on.
  • Price levels: the demo company has Wholesale (-5%), VIP (-10%), Retail Plus (+5%), Price Level 4 to Price Level 10 (0%, ready to be renamed). Fixed prices per item and level are set on the item or in Set Item Prices and Levels (see Inventory).
  • Sales representatives: with their commission %, used by the Sales by Rep reports.
  • Customer types and Billing groups (billing groups are used by Create Batch Invoices).

The accounts every customer transaction uses (Accounts Receivable, Undeposited Funds, Default Sales Income, Sales Returns and Allowances, Sales Discounts, Finance Charge Income, Reimbursable Expense Income, Creditable Withholding Tax, Creditable VAT Withheld and Output Tax) are set in Module Default Accounts. If one is missing, saving stops with a message such as "The default account "Accounts Receivable" is not set. Set it under Financial Set-up & Reports > Module Default Accounts."

The finance charge rate, minimum charge and grace period are set in Company, Security & Closing Date. The discount and price limits of each role, and whether a role may exceed credit limits, are set in User Role Configuration.

Customer Center

The Customer Center is where you keep customer and job records and where you see everything about one customer: balance, credit limit, transactions, notes and reminders. It is also the quickest place to start any sale for a customer, because every form opens with the customer already filled in.

Where to find it: Customers > Customer Center

The customer list

The left side lists your customers in alphabetical order, each job indented under its customer with an arrow. The figure beside each name is that record's own Accounts Receivable balance.

  • Type to find a customer or job: the list narrows as you type, letter by letter, to the customers and jobs whose name, company name, contact person, phone or mobile contains what you typed. The count under the box says how many are shown ("3 of 24 customer(s) and job(s)"). When nothing matches, the list says No customer matches. Press Enter to search the whole list on the server. Press Enter to open the first match, or, when the list on screen is filtered to active customers, to search the whole list, inactive ones included.
  • The drop-down beside it shows Active customers (the default), All customers including inactive ones (marked "inactive"), or only those With balance.
  • Click a name to show its details on the right.

At the top of the page, New customer / job opens a blank customer form and Export list downloads the list shown as a spreadsheet file (CSV) with the name, parent customer, company, contact, phone, email, billing address, terms, credit limit, balance and active flag. Exporting needs the print permission.

A customer's details

The top card shows the name (as Customer:Job for a job), the company name and TIN, the contact person, phone, mobile and email, and the billing address. On the right are:

  • Open balance: what the customer owes today. For a customer, it includes the balances of its jobs.
  • Credit limit: the limit, or "none".
  • Available credits: shown in green when the customer has unapplied payments or credit memos.

Below are the Terms, Price level (Standard when none is set), Tax, Rep, Type and Pays by (preferred payment method).

Step by step

To add a customer:

  1. Click New customer / job.
  2. Type the Customer / job name and leave Job of blank.
  3. Fill in the company, contact, addresses, TIN and the terms, credit limit, price level and other settings.
  4. Click Save customer, or Save customer & New to go on with the next one.

To add a job:

  1. Select the customer in the list.
  2. Click Add job. The form opens with the customer in Job of.
  3. Type the job name and its own address, terms and settings.
  4. Click Save customer.

To change a customer or its credit limit:

  1. Select the customer and click Edit.
  1. Change what you need.
  2. If you changed Credit limit (0 = none), type the reason in Reason for change (required for credit limit changes).
  3. Click Save customer.

To add a note or a to-do reminder:

  1. Select the customer.
  2. In the Notes and to-do reminders card, type the note in Add a note or reminder.
  3. Optionally pick a date, and tick To-do if it is something to follow up.
  4. Click Add. When the task is done, click Done beside it.

Good to know

  • Customers are never deleted. Untick Active instead; the history stays and the customer disappears from the drop-downs of new forms.
  • Two customers cannot have the same name, and two jobs of one customer cannot either: "Another customer or job already has this name."
  • A credit limit change without a reason stops with "Enter a reason for changing the credit limit." Users with the Approve permission on the Customer Center, and administrators, may leave the reason blank. Every change is kept in the audit trail with the values before and after.
  • Adding notes needs the edit permission on the Customer Center.
  • The price level and sales representative of the record chosen on a form are used, so a job can have its own price level.

Item lines

The lines grid holds what you sell. Three blank lines are ready (one on a statement charge); click Add line, Add 5 lines or Add 10 lines for more, the square plus button at the end of a line to insert an empty line above it, and the red X to remove a line. The Inventory site chosen at the top is copied onto every line.

Column What to enter / what it means

Description Filled with the item's sales description. You may change it.
Qty The quantity, in the unit shown in U/M. Set to 1 when you pick an item.
U/M The unit of measure. Shown only when an item on the form has more than one unit.

How the item list shows each item (code first or name first) is set under "Searching for items" in Company, Security & Closing Date, for all forms or for each form separately. Whichever way it is shown, typing any part of the code or the name finds the item. Only active items are offered.

If you type an Amount yourself on a line that has a quantity and no discount, the program works the Rate back from it. On a line with no quantity and no rate, the amount you type is used as it is.

Every line with a description or an amount needs an item: "Choose an item for every line that has a description or amount." A form with no lines stops with "Add at least one line."

Prices and price levels

When you pick an item, the rate is the item's sales price for the customer chosen on the form:

  • A customer with no price level (Standard) gets the item's sales price.
  • A customer with a price level gets the fixed price set for that item and level, if there is one.
  • Otherwise the sales price is changed by the level's percentage. With VIP (-10%), an item selling at ₱899.00 is offered at ₱809.10.

Choose the customer before the items. Lines already on the form keep their rates if you change the customer afterwards. See the Item Price List for the price of every item at every level.

Units of measure

Items that belong to a U/M set can be sold in any unit of the set (see Inventory). The U/M column starts on the item's default sales unit. Changing the unit re-prices the line for the new unit. Stock always moves in the base unit.

Example

Metro School Supplies buys 3 dz (dozen) Keyboard USB at ₱11,000.00 a dozen, with ₱1,500.00 off (Amt). The line amount is 3 × ₱11,000.00 less ₱1,500.00 = ₱31,500.00, and 36 pc leave the warehouse.

Discounts on a line

Type the discount in the Discount column and choose % or Amt beside it. A percent cannot be more than 100, and an amount off can never be more than the line itself. The printout shows the discount in the description, for example "(less 1,500.00)".

Special items

Besides service, inventory, non-inventory, assembly and other charge items, four kinds of items behave differently on a sales form:

  • Discount item: with a percentage, it takes that percent off the line just above it; without one, it takes off the amount in its Rate (or its sales price). Its amount is negative.
  • Subtotal item: adds up the lines above it, back to the previous subtotal, and shows the total under its description. Put a discount item right after a subtotal to discount a group of lines.
  • Group item: a set of items sold together at its own Group price (see Item groups on sales documents).
  • Payment item: money received on the document itself. Type the amount in Rate; it reduces the total due and is recorded in Undeposited Funds.

Item groups on sales documents

An item group (for example GRP-WFH Work-from-Home Bundle: a laptop, a mouse and a backpack) is sold on one line of an estimate, sales order, invoice, sales receipt, statement charge or credit memo, and at the POS. Pick the group: Qty is the number of groups and Rate is the price of one group, its Group price (with the customer's price level), or the sum of the prices of its items when Adopt the Group Price in the Item Group is cleared or the group has no group price (see Item groups: group price and price variance). A discount on the line is taken off the group.

When the document is saved, the group line is recorded as the items in it, each at its own price, so stock, cost of sales and the sales of each item are kept as always, plus one line Price variance: Work-from-Home Bundle for the group price less the prices of its items, posted to the group's Price variance GL account. The document total is the group price times the quantity. A group sold for less than its price, or at another price, needs the same supervisor approval as an item.

A document with item groups has two buttons above its lines, on its page and on its edit form:

Button What it shows
View by item group One line per group: the group, how many, its price and amount, and under the description the items it holds. Editing in this view changes the group as a whole: a new quantity or price re-prices all of its items and its variance, and the saved lines keep their place (what was invoiced from a sales order stays linked).
View item details Every item of each group on a line of its own, at its own price, with the price variance line and "In item group ..." under the description. Each line can be edited on its own.

When Track Item Group Sales transactions is ticked (System Administration > Company, Security & Closing Date > Inventory and POS), each group sold is also kept apart as a group, for Sales by Item Group and POS Sales by Item Group.

Tax and totals

The box at the bottom right shows Subtotal, Tax and Total, and changes as you type. Tax is worked out per line: the line amount times the rate of its tax code. The printed document shows "Subtotal (net of VAT)", "VAT" and "Total".

Supervisor approval

Estimates, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts and batch invoices check every line against the limits of your role (set in User Role Configuration):

  • Max discount % without approval: applies to a line discount (a percent, or an amount off counted as a percent of the line) and to the percentage of a discount item.
  • Max price change % without approval: a rate that differs from the customer's price by more than this percent.

A blank limit on the role means no limit. If a line goes over, saving stops with a message such as "Supervisor approval is required: 15.00% discount on LAP-001. Ask a supervisor to enter their username and password below." and the Supervisor approval box opens, marked "required". The supervisor types their Supervisor username and Password there, and you save again. The supervisor must have the Approve permission on the page and cannot approve their own document. Their name is recorded as Approved by on the document and on the printout. A user who has the Approve permission on the page does not need anyone else.

Other messages you may see: "Approval failed: the supervisor username or password is wrong." (the failed attempt is logged) and "(name) is not allowed to approve on this page."

Example

In the demo company the A/R Clerk role may give up to 10% discount and change prices by up to 5% without approval.

Class

When classes are set up, the Class box appears on every form. If you save without choosing one, the program asks: "This transaction has no class. It will be saved as Unclassified and will not appear under any class on the class reports." Click Choose a class to go back, or Continue saving.

Saving

  • Save saves and opens the saved document.
  • Save & New saves and opens a blank form for the next one.

If something is wrong, the form comes back with the message in red at the top and nothing is saved. Correct it and save again. A saved document is posted at once; editing it later removes the old ledger entries and stock movements and records the new ones, so the books always match the document.

Printing and emailing

Print opens the document on a clean page with Print and Back buttons on top. Click Print to send it to a printer or save it as PDF from your browser. The printout carries the company name, address, TIN and phone (with the logo when the administrator switched it on), the document title and number, the date, the due date or "Valid until", the customer's name, billing address and TIN, the ship-to address, reference, terms, site and class, the lines, the customer message and memo, the totals ("Balance due" on invoices and charges), and signature lines for Prepared by, Checked by, Approved by and Received by. Email opens a new message in your own e-mail program, addressed to the customer's email, with a subject such as "Invoice INV-00002 from Sample Trading Company" and a link to the printable copy. Memorizing

Memorize keeps a copy of the document to enter again later, for example a monthly service invoice. The dialog asks for Name it, Group (optional), How it is entered (Enter it automatically, Remind me when it is due, or Don't remind me), Every (a number and Day(s), Week(s), Month(s), Quarter(s) or Year(s)), Next due, Remind (days before), Number remaining (blank = no limit) and Notes. The names, items and amounts are kept; the date and number are given each time. See Memorized Transactions.

Voiding and deleting

Both ask for a Reason (required, recorded in the audit trail).

  • Void keeps the document, marked VOID with the reason and time, and removes its effect on the ledger and the inventory. Its status becomes Void.
  • Delete removes the document. A full copy stays in the audit trail.

Estimates and sales orders never post, so they have only Delete. The program refuses to void or delete a document when:

  • payments or credits were applied to it: "Payments or credits have been applied to it. Remove them first (edit the payment or credit)."
  • it was included in a bank deposit: "It is included in deposit DEP-00001. Remove it from the deposit first."
  • a credit memo or payment was refunded: "It has been refunded (…). Void the refund first."
  • an estimate or sales order was already converted in part: "It has already been converted or received in part. Close it instead."

Rules for every form

  • The closing date: without the permission "Can change transactions on/before the closing date or already reconciled", you cannot enter, change, void or delete a document dated on or before the closing date: "The books are closed through 08/31/2026. You do not have permission to record transactions dated on or before the closing date."
  • A document cleared in a bank reconciliation cannot be changed by users without that permission.
  • Users limited to certain inventory sites only see and use those sites: "You are not allowed to record transactions for that site."
  • Inactive customers and items are not offered on new forms.
  • Every save, edit, void, delete, print and e-mail is recorded in the audit trail.

Create Estimates

An estimate is a price quotation. Use it when a customer asks how much something will cost before ordering. It does not affect the books or the stock, and when the customer accepts, you turn it into a sales order or an invoice with one click, without typing the lines again.

Where to find it: Customers > Create Estimates. Also Estimate in the Customer Center, and Create Estimates under Quick actions on the Home dashboard (opens a new form).

The list's Status filter offers Pending, Accepted and Closed.

Fields

The common fields are explained under Working with sales forms, earlier in this chapter. The estimate adds:

Field What to enter / what it means
Reference Any reference, for example the customer's inquiry number.
Valid until How long the quoted prices hold. 30 days from today by default. Printed on the estimate.
Status Pending (waiting for the customer), Accepted or Closed.

The lines grid has the Discount column. The Supervisor approval box is below the lines.

Step by step

To prepare an estimate:

  1. Click New.
  2. Choose the Customer:Job. Bill to, Ship to and Sales rep fill in.
  3. Check the Date and Valid until.
  4. Add the lines: choose each item, then correct the quantity, rate or discount if needed.
  5. Type a Memo if useful, for example "Office equipment for 5 workstations".
  6. Click Save.

To send it, open the estimate and click Print or Email.

To turn an accepted estimate into a sales order or an invoice:

  1. Open the estimate.
  2. Click Create Sales Order or Create Invoice. The new form opens with a blue note "Creating from Estimate EST-00001." and with the customer, addresses, sales rep, class and lines of the estimate, including their tax. The memo reads "From Estimate EST-00001".
  3. Change what is different, for example the quantity the customer really ordered.
  4. Click Save.

To bill an estimate in stages (progress billing), create the invoice from the estimate and lower the quantities or amounts to the part you are billing now. The estimate remembers how much of each line has been invoiced. The next invoice made from it leaves out the lines already invoiced in full, but brings any other line at its full estimate quantity, so lower it again to the part being billed.

To close an estimate the customer declined, open it and click Close, then confirm "Close this document? Remaining quantities will no longer be expected." Reopen puts it back to Pending.

What it records

Nothing. An estimate is a non-posting document ("Non-posting document: it records a commitment and does not affect the ledger."). It does not move stock.

The status changes by itself:

  • Creating a sales order from a Pending estimate makes it Accepted.
  • Invoicing part of it makes it Accepted; invoicing every line in full makes it Closed.
  • Close makes it Closed; Reopen makes it Pending.

Good to know

  • Estimates cannot be voided, only deleted, and only while nothing has been created from them. After that, use Close.
  • Discounts and price changes above your limits need supervisor approval, even on an estimate, so a quotation cannot promise a price the company would not approve.
  • The Related documents card lists every sales order and invoice made from the estimate.

Create Sales Orders

A sales order records what the customer ordered and you have promised to deliver. Use it when goods are delivered or billed later, or in parts. The order tracks how much of each line has been invoiced, so back orders are never forgotten.

Where to find it: Customers > Create Sales Orders. Also Sales order in the Customer Center, Create Sales Order on an estimate, and Create Sales Orders under Quick actions on the Home dashboard.

The list's Status filter offers Open, Partially filled, Closed and Void.

Fields

The common fields are explained under Working with sales forms, earlier in this chapter. The sales order adds:

Field What to enter / what it means
Customer P.O. No. The customer's purchase order number. Carried to the invoice.
Expected ship date When you expect to deliver. Shown on the Open Sales Orders reports.
Inventory site The warehouse or store the goods will come from.

When you edit a saved order, the grid has two more columns: Invoiced (the quantity already invoiced) and Closed (tick to stop expecting the rest of that line). A line that has been invoiced in part has no X: it cannot be removed, only closed.

Step by step

To enter a sales order:

  1. Click New, or click Create Sales Order on the estimate.
  2. Choose the Customer:Job and type the Customer P.O. No.
  3. Set the Expected ship date and the Inventory site.
  4. Enter or check the lines.
  5. Click Save.

To invoice what you deliver:

  1. Open the sales order and click Create Invoice (shown while the order is Open or Partial).
  2. The invoice opens with every line not yet fully invoiced, at the quantity still open. Closed lines are left out.
  3. Lower the quantities to what you are delivering now, or remove the lines you are not delivering.
  4. Click Save. The order's Invoiced column goes up and its status becomes Partial, or Closed when everything is invoiced.

To stop expecting the rest of an order, open it and click Close (all lines), or edit it and tick Closed on single lines. Reopen opens all lines again.

What it records

Nothing in the ledger ("Non-posting document"). The quantities not yet invoiced count as "on sales order" in the inventory reports and in Open Sales Orders by Item, which helps purchasing see what is needed.

The status is set by the program: Open (nothing invoiced), Partial (some lines invoiced) and Closed (every line invoiced or closed).

Good to know

  • A line already used on an invoice cannot be removed: "A line that was already used on another document cannot be removed. Close the line instead."

Assess Finance Charges

Assess Finance Charges works out interest on overdue balances for every customer and posts it as finance charges. Run it at the end of a month if your terms allow you to charge interest on late payments.

Where to find it: Customers > Assess Finance Charges, or Assess Finance Charges under Quick actions on the Home dashboard.

The box at the top shows the settings in use, for example "Annual interest rate 12.00%, minimum charge 50.00, grace period 0 days, earlier finance charges are not charged interest. Interest runs from the due date (or the last assessment) to the assessment date. A customer already charged on or after the assessment date is not charged again." They are changed with Finance charge settings, which opens Company, Security & Closing Date (Annual interest rate %, Minimum finance charge, Grace period (days) and Charge interest on earlier finance charges).

The columns

Column What it means
Checkbox Ticked customers are charged. All are ticked at first.
Customer The customer. Jobs are included in their customer.
Overdue documents Each overdue document and the days of interest counted.
Last charged The date of the customer's last finance charge.
Overdue balance The open amount of those documents.
Finance charge The interest worked out. You may change it.

How the charge is worked out

For each open invoice, statement charge (and earlier finance charge, if that setting is on) whose due date plus the grace period is before the assessment date:

  • interest = open amount × annual rate ÷ 100 × days ÷ 365
  • the days run from the due date, or from the last finance charge if that is later, to the assessment date.
  • a customer who already has a finance charge dated on or after the assessment date is left out: that period has been charged.

The interest of all the customer's documents is added up, and a customer's charge is never less than the minimum charge.

Example

On 09/27/2026, INV-00001 of ABC Trading Corp. has ₱81,832.80 open and has been due for 20 days. At 12% a year: ₱81,832.80 × 12% × 20 ÷ 365 = ₱538.08. Green Valley Clinic was last charged on 09/26/2026, so only 1 day counts; its interest is below the minimum, so it is charged ₱50.00.

Step by step

  1. Type the Assessment date and click Recalculate.
  1. Untick the customers you will not charge.
  2. Change a Finance charge amount if you agreed on another figure.
  3. Choose a Class if you use classes.
  4. Click Assess charges.

The program shows "N finance charge(s) posted." and opens the list of finance charges.

Posted finance charges opens this list at any time. Click a number to open a finance charge; from there you can print, email, void or delete it.

What it records

One finance charge per ticked customer, numbered FC-…, dated and due on the assessment date, with the memo "Finance charge on overdue balance as of …" and a line naming the documents charged:

Account Debit Credit
1100 Accounts Receivable ₱538.08
4100 Finance Charge Income ₱538.08

Finance charges appear on the next statement, in the aging reports and in Receive Payments.

Good to know

  • If nobody is overdue, the page shows "No customers have overdue balances as of …".
  • "Select at least one customer to charge." appears if you click Assess charges with every customer unticked or every amount at zero.
  • A customer is never charged twice for the same period. After an assessment, the customers charged no longer appear for that date or any earlier one, and if the page is sent again (a double click, or a second user assessing at the same time) the program posts nothing more for them and says "Not charged again, already charged on or after …" with their names. To undo a charge, void it; the customer then appears again.
  • A saved finance charge cannot be changed, so it has no Edit button. To correct a charge, void it and assess again, or enter a statement charge for the difference.

Create Credit Memos/Refunds

A credit memo reduces what a customer owes: for goods returned, a price correction or an allowance. The credit can be kept for later, applied to an invoice at once, or paid back as a refund. This page also records refunds of overpayments.

Where to find it: Customers > Create Credit Memos/Refunds. Also Credit memo in the Customer Center, and Create Credit Memos/Refunds under Quick actions on the Home dashboard.

Step by step

To record returned goods:

  1. Click New.
  2. Choose the Customer:Job. The page reloads for the customer.
  3. Leave Return goods to stock ticked if the goods can be sold again, and choose Return to site if they go to another site. Untick it for defective goods that will not return to stock.
  4. Enter the items, quantities and rates returned, with the same tax code as the sale.
  5. Type a Memo such as "Returned 2 defective keyboards".
  6. In Credit, choose what to do with it:
    • Keep as available credit: the customer uses it on a later payment.
    • Apply to an invoice now: choose the Invoice; the credit reduces it by up to its open balance.
    • Give a refund: after saving, the refund form opens ("Now record how the refund was paid.").
  7. Click Save.

To apply a kept credit later, open the credit memo and click Apply credit to invoices. Receive Payments opens with all the customer's available credits spread over the open invoices; change the amounts if needed and click Save payment. See Receive Payments.

To refund a credit memo or an overpayment:

  1. Open the credit memo or payment and click Refund (shown while it has unapplied credit).
  2. The page "Refund Credit Memo CM-00001" shows the customer and the available credit. Fill in:
    • Date.
    • Pay from account: the bank account the refund is paid from. Required.
    • Refund method: cash, check and so on.
    • Amount: the available credit by default; you may refund less.
    • Memo: "Refund of Credit Memo CM-00001" by default.
    • Class, when classes are set up.
    • Queue a refund check for printing: tick if a check will be printed.
  3. Click Record refund. The program shows "Refund recorded." and opens the refund.

The refund takes the next check number and prints as a REFUND VOUCHER with the payee, address, TIN, amount in words, the account paid from, the ledger lines and signature lines including Received by.

What it records

A credit memo is the reverse of a sale. Each line is debited to 4050 Sales Returns and Allowances (whatever item it is), its tax is debited to the output tax account, and the total is credited to 1100 Accounts Receivable. With Return goods to stock ticked, stocked items come back into the site at the current average cost: debit Inventory, credit Cost of Goods Sold.

Example

CM-00001 credits Metro School Supplies for 2 keyboards returned to stock.

Account Debit Credit
4050 Sales Returns and Allowances ₱1,898.10
2100 Output Tax ₱227.77
1200 Inventory ₱1,233.33
5000 Cost of Goods Sold ₱1,233.33
1100 Accounts Receivable ₱2,125.87

A refund pays the credit back:

Account Debit Credit
1100 Accounts Receivable Refund amount
Pay from account (bank) Refund amount

The credit memo's status is Open while it has unapplied credit and Closed when it is fully applied or refunded.

Good to know

  • Refund messages: "The refund must be more than zero and no more than the available credit (…)." and "Choose the account the refund is paid from."
  • A refund above your role's "Max check / bill payment / adjustment amount without approval" needs supervisor approval ("refund above your amount limit"). Voiding or deleting a refund above that amount needs approval too.
  • Refunds cannot be edited: "Refunds cannot be edited. Void it and record a new one."
  • A credit memo that was refunded cannot be voided until the refund is voided: "It has been refunded (…). Void the refund first." Voiding a credit memo that was applied removes the application and reopens the invoice.
  • The What to do with this credit card only appears on a new credit memo. Discount and price limits are not checked on credit memos.
